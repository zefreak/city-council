 3   2026 Mid-Year Budget Supplemental

 4

 5   WHEREAS, RCW 36.40.071 authorizes the Clark County Council to meet on or before the first
 6   Monday in December for the purpose of hearing and considering testimony for or against any part
 7   of the proposed final budget; and

 8   WHEREAS, RCW 36.40.080 requires the Clark County Council to fix the budget, by resolution,
 9   at the conclusion of the budget hearing; and

10   WHEREAS, the Washington State Budgeting, Accounting and Reporting System (BARS) manual
11   states, "budgets showing revenues and expenditures at the legal fund level to be the minimum
12   acceptable level of detail;" and

13   WHEREAS, there are alternative controls that can be implemented to maintain proper, legal
14   review of all county funds and expenditures therefrom; and

15   WHEREAS, detailed budget preparation will remain an important part of the county's
16   management and planning process; and

17   WHEREAS, all appropriations lapse at the end of the budget year, except for capital projects
18   appropriations which may continue through the approved multi-year appropriation established per
19   project.

20   BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE CLARK COUNTY COUNCIL FOR CLARK
21   COUNTY, STATE OF WASHINGTON, as follows:

22   Section I.     The Clark County Council hereby finds that a supplemental appropriation for the
23   2026 annual budget is necessary as set forth herein.

24   Section 2.     The amounts as to each impacted fund(s) and/or department(s) are set forth in
25   Exhibit "A", which is attached hereto and incorporated herein by this reference.
26   Section 3.    The Council hereby approves and adopts this supplemental appropriation for the
27   2026 annual budget.

28   Section 4.    Copies of this resolution shall be filed with the Clark County Auditor, the affected
29   department(s), and in the records of the Clark County Council for Clark County, Washington.

30   Adopted on this _ _ day of September 2026.
