Requested Action:
This Change Request implements the Council-approved appropriations authorized through the Staff Report No.174-26 approved on July 21, 2026, for the implementation of HB 2015
Washington State Criminal Justice Training Commission grant funding. The 2026 Mid-Year Budget Supplemental provides appropriation authority for expenditures supported through
the HB 2015 Local Law Enforcement Grant Program (Round 1), along with the remaining funding necessary to support Council-approved positions funded through the Councilmanic
0.1 percent Criminal Justice Sales and Use Tax.

This request recognizes the Council-approved 2026 full-time equivalent (FTE) positions and associated operating expenditures and provides the budget authority necessary to
implement these approvals during the remainder of 2026. Consistent with the Council-approved grant implementation strategy, additional position authority and related appropriations
for future phases of implementation will be incorporated through the 2027 and 2028 annual budget processes.


Justification:
Pursuant to RCW 43.101.540 (ESHB 2015), the Washington State Criminal Justice Training Commission established a $100 million grant program, available through June 30, 2028,
to support local and tribal law enforcement agencies in hiring, retaining, and training law enforcement personnel, peer counselors, and behavioral health professionals serving in co-
response roles.

Clark County was awarded Round 1 grant funding to support public safety initiatives during implementation of the voter-approved 0.1% Criminal Justice Sales and Use Tax. This
funding is intended to supplement local resources and accelerate Council-approved public safety investments by supporting recruitment and retention efforts, expanding staffing
capacity within the Sheriff's Office and Jail, enhancing co-response partnerships, providing required training, and purchasing essential equipment. The grant advances the County's
strategic objective of strengthening community policing and public safety while leveraging state funding to offset eligible implementation costs associated with HB 2015.


Cost Estimate/Comments:

Cost estimates have been budgeted consistent with the funding plan presented in Staff Report No. 174-26, as approved by Council on July 21, 2026. The Budget Office will continue
to coordinate with the Auditor's Office, Sheriff's Office, and Jail Services Department to ensure that any eligible unspent 2026 grant funding is carried forward into 2027 in accordance
with grant requirements and County budget policies.

This Change Request provides appropriation authority for the anticipated 2026 grant revenue of $1,626,698. Appropriation authority for anticipated grant revenues in 2027 and 2028
will be incorporated through the respective annual budget processes to align with the approved multi-year grant implementation strategy.

This Change Request provides appropriation authority for fourteen (14) additional FTE, including the associated one-time startup costs and operating expenses:

-10 Deputy Sheriff Positions (Sheriff's Office)
-4 Corrections Officer Positions (Jail Services Department)

For 2026, the estimated salary and benefit cost for these positions is $738,266, reflecting a partial-year cost based on an anticipated August 2026 start date. Consistent with the
approved funding strategy, 75 percent of these costs will be supported through the HB 2015 Local Law Enforcement Grant Program, with the remaining 25 percent funded by the
Councilmanic 0.1 percent Criminal Justice Sales and Use Tax.

Beginning in 2027, the estimated annual salary and benefit cost for these positions is $1,787,742. Through the grant period, 75 percent of these ongoing personnel costs will continue
to be funded by the HB 2015 Local Law Enforcement Grant Program, while the remaining 25 percent will be supported by the Councilmanic 0.1 percent Criminal Justice Sales and
Use Tax. Upon expiration of the grant in 2028, the dedicated sales and use tax will assume responsibility for the ongoing costs of these Council-approved positions, consistent with
the long-term funding strategy approved in Staff Report No. 098-26 on April 21, 2026. Annual personnel cost estimates will continue to be refined through the County's budget
development process to reflect approved compensation adjustments, benefit rate changes, staffing assumptions, and other personnel-related cost factors.

In addition to personnel costs, this Change Request includes $1,046,590 in one-time implementation expenditures for equipment, supplies, training, overtime,, and other eligible
startup expenses necessary to support the deployment of the Council-approved positions and implementation of the HB 2015 grant program. Fleet-related costs will be submitted in
the 2027 budget request to align with the vehicle acquisition timeline.


Impacts/Outcomes:
This request has received prior Council approval.
