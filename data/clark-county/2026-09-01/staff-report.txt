        BACKGROUND

        Background:
        The Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) and the Home Investment Partnerships Program (HOME)
        are two federal grant programs administered by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development
        (HUD). These programs provide annual allocations to carry out community and economic development
        activities, improve affordable housing options, and provide improved community facilities and services. CDBG
        and HOME activities must meet several HUD requirements and must be used to benefit households with low-
        to moderate- income. The CDBG funds may only be used for projects that benefit households at or below 80%
        of area median income and HOME funds primarily benefit households at or below 60% of area median income.

        State and local governments that receive CDBG and HOME funds are required to provide HUD with a five-
        year Consolidated Plan, an Annual Action Plan, and a Consolidated Annual Performance and Evaluation Report
        (CAPER). The Council approved the 2025-2029 Consolidated Plan and 2025 Annual Action Plan on May 6,
        2025. The CAPER, which does not require Council approval, describes program activity which occurred during
        the 2025 Action Plan program year, July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2026.

        To ensure public participation in CDBG and HOME activities, HUD requires at least two public hearings each
        year to obtain residents’ views on housing and community economic development needs, proposed activities
        and program performance. Community Services is requesting a public hearing to receive public comments
        regarding needed programs and projects in communities throughout Clark County outside Vancouver city limits
        and the 2025 CAPER prior to submission to HUD. Submission of the CAPER is due to HUD by September 28,
        60 days from the close of the program year on June 30. A public hearing will be scheduled in the spring to
        review new proposed activities recommended through the PY2027 request for application process.

        COMMUNITY OUTREACH
        The Urban County Policy Board developed and implemented the five-year Consolidated Plan and Annual
        Action Plan in an open process that included participation from service providers, public agencies and
        organizations and County citizens. All Urban County Policy Board meetings are open to the public and are
        scheduled at times and locations convenient to potential and actual beneficiaries with accommodations for
        people with disabilities and oral language assistance (interpretation) for non-English speaking persons. Those
        wishing to be regularly informed of meetings/activities can be added to the program’s e-mail list.
        Notifications for requests for public comments are published in The Columbian, sent out through a Clark Co.
        Communications News Release, posted on the county’s Facebook, X and LinkedIn social media accounts,
        posted on Clark County Community Services website and emailed to a stakeholders list of over 400
        recipients including local governments, nonprofit organizations and community members. The Consolidated
        Plan, Annual Action Plan, and Consolidated Annual Performance and Evaluation Reports are also posted
        online for public review prior to a public hearing and subsequent approval by the County Council.
