                                                               Slide 3
     Task 5 – Broad Area Site Screening (BASS)
158 possible sites were identified in initial screening
   • Aggregating up to 5 parcels per site

Initial list was reduced to 15 sites using primary screening criteria
   • Not residentially zoned
   • Not in flood zone
   • No less than 20 usable acres
   • Within 1 mile of essential utilities
   • Compliant with FAA regulations

                                                                        Slide 4
       Task 5 – Broad Area Site Screening (BASS)

The 15 site list was reduced to 7 sites through detailed site review
and screening process

   •    Desk top studies were conducted for wetlands/habitat,
        geotechnical, cultural and historic resources and traffic

   •    Two sites are located within City of Ridgefield

Sites with “potentially” willing sellers were identified (5 out of 7)

                                                                   Slide 7
         BASS Results & Recommendations

•   Four sites have reasonably similar scoring: 12/13, 116, 137 and 141
    and are recommended for further consideration
•   Two sites (11 and 127) are located within the City of Ridgefield, are
    zoned commercial/employment and are not recommended for
    further consideration
•   Site 117 scored the lowest, has the second highest cost of varying
    cost centers, and is not recommended for further consideration
                                                                     Slide 8
        BASS Results & Recommendations

•   Further site study in a focused area site screening is not likely to
    score sites 12/13, 116, 137 and 141 differently

•   Recommend further investigation of the interest level of the
    sellers for sites 12/13, 116, 137 and 141

•   Recommend moving to an environmental assessment (EIS) for
    the two sites with the strongest likelihood of willing sellers

Transfer Station Location Considerations:
   Customer access and convenience a main consideration

   Transfer Station Siting Rule of Thumb – 10 miles minimum separation

   Traffic conditions a major consideration for locating transfer stations

   Counties are solely responsible for planning and financing essential solid
     waste facilities for their citizens
   Regionalization of solid waste facilities and services in Washington has
    not occurred for good reasons
   Regionalization (multi-county) can work for essential public services in
    areas with sufficient population such as Portland Metro
