              Key Decision Considerations
• Address immediate and emerging 2026 budget needs.

• Maintain flexibility.

• Ongoing impacts versus new or expanded commitments.

• Preserve available General Fund.

• Align funding with updated revenue estimates and available funding sources.

• Avoid decisions that could limit options or create additional pressure for 2027 and
  future years.
