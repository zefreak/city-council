Background
• The Columbia River Gorge Commission was established in 1987 through
  congressional adoption of the Columbia River Gorge National Scenic
  Area Act.

• The Commission’s mission is to establish, implement and enforce
  policies and programs that protect the National Scenic Area.

• In 1991 the Gorge Commission and the Forest Service adopted a
  Management Plan which helps keep consistent standards and
  regulations within the Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area and
  under Clark County Code 40.240

Proposed Guideline C
• Replacement structures that are in the same location, size, footprint, and
  height qualify for expedited review.

• Guidelines C.4 and C.5 allow for some flexibility.

• Landowners have 10 years to commence construction (no interim period to
  apply).

• Limited review – only color, reflectivity, and landscaping (specific
  consideration for defensible space).

• Gorge Commission staff will develop an advisory handbook.

 Proposed Guideline D
• Temporary use of recreational vehicles

• Temporary structures
    • Limited to 320 square feet
    • Place outside of resource areas and buffers
    • No foundation or grading
    • Dark earth-tone colors (prescribed a paint color to facilitate ministerial review)
    • Document continuing need every two years

• RVs and temporary structures require approval (may be ministerial, checklist-type
  review)

 Proposed Guideline E
• Non – residential uses, (e.g., home occupations) are not considered
  discontinued solely because the use is inactive while rebuilding the
  structure where the use occurred.

• Exceptions:
   • Special uses in historic buildings
   • Bed and breakfast inns on a parcel in the SMA
   • Overnight accommodations
   • Commerial events

 Proposed Guideline F
