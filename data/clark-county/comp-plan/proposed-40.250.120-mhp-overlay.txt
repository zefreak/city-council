                                       40.250 OVERLAY DISTRICTS


40.250.120   Manufactured Home Park Overlay District (MHP)
A.    Purpose.

    The Manufactured Home Park Overlay District (MHP) is intended to preserve existing
manufactured home parks, protect long-term affordability, and prevent the displacement of
existing residents. The district promotes community stability by limiting redevelopment of
manufactured home parks while allowing the continued repair, replacement, and expansion of
manufactured housing in accordance with the standards of this title.

B.        Applicability.

     1.    The MHP Overlay District shall be applied to existing and new manufactured home
     parks within urban growth areas of Clark County. Once a property has been developed as a
     manufactured home park, a docket is required to change the zoning to apply the MHP overlay
     zone.

     2.      The standards contained in this district shall supersede the base zone standards.

C.        Uses.

     Permitted uses in the MHP Overlay District are limited to the following:

     1.      Manufactured home parks, subject to Section 40.260.140.

     2.    Residential uses permitted in the base zone, if developed as affordable housing consistent
     with subsection E.

     3.      Transitional housing.

     4.      Permanent supportive housing.

     5.   Accessory uses and structures normal to a residential environment, subject to Section
     40.260.010.

     6.      Family day care centers, subject to Section 40.260.160.

     7.      Adult family homes, subject to Section 40.260.190.

     8.      Home business – Type I, subject to Section 40.260.100.

     9.      Garage sales, subject to Section 40.260.090.

     10.      Temporary uses, subject to Section 40.260.220.

     11.      Utilities, other than wireless communications facilities, subject to Section 40.260.240.

     12.      Wireless communications facilities, subject to Section 40.260.250.

     13.      Electric vehicle infrastructure, subject to Section 40.260.075.

D.        Standards.

     1.      Manufactured home parks shall be subject to the standards of Section 40.260.140.

     2.   All other uses shall be subject to the standards of the base zone and applicable special use
     standards.

E.        Use Exception.

   A property owner within the MHP Overlay District may seek a comprehensive plan map and
zone change for the property pursuant to the procedures set forth in Section 40.560.020. The
applicant must demonstrate that:

     1.      The existing MHP Overlay District does not allow for reasonable use of the property;

     2.   The uses permitted in the MHP Overlay District are not economically viable due to the
     property’s location, site conditions, or surrounding development; or

     3. The applicant proposes to redevelop the site with affordable housing, and proposes to
     assist existing residents to minimize displacement including relocation assistance, right to
     return policies for the proposed affordable housing, or other assistance.
