County to host manufactured home park information session

Vancouver, Wash. – Clark County community members are invited to attend an information session on
proposals relating to manufactured home parks in unincorporated Clark County on Sept. 14 at 6:30pm.

In August 2025, the Clark County Council declared a moratorium to prohibit the redevelopment of
existing manufactured home parks (MHPs) within its jurisdiction. The moratorium was renewed through
February 2027 to allow time to develop strategies to better support preservation of existing MHPs, to
help maintain this affordable housing option, and to minimize resident displacement.

As part of the Comprehensive Growth Management Plan update project, the county is considering
changes to comprehensive plan policies and the addition of a new MHP overlay zone within
unincorporated urban growth areas. The purpose of the overlay is to prioritize MHP uses and limit other
residential uses to reduce redevelopment pressure. The proposal is similar to zoning tools used in
several Washington cities, including the City of Vancouver, looking to preserve MHPs and minimize
displacement of park residents.

An information session on the proposals relating to MHPs in the unincorporated urban growth areas will
begin with a presentation followed by a question and answer session. Options are available to attend
in-person or remotely:
    • Date: Monday, September 14, 2026
    • Time: 6:30-7:30pm. Doors open at 6:00pm
    • Join in-person: Public Service Center, 6th floor, Room 680, 1300 Franklin St, Vancouver
    • Join remotely: Login details will be posted to https://bit.ly/2025CPMtg
