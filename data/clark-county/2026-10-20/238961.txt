        BACKGROUND
        The existing single lane, all-way stop-controlled intersection at Northeast 179th Street and Northeast 29th
        Avenue will be replaced with a roundabout-controlled intersection. This project (PRJ0000580) is currently
        under construction and includes construction of a roundabout, seven retaining walls, two bridge creek
        crossings, streambed reconstruction, waterline, sanitary sewer, stormwater and ped/bike infrastructure. This
        project improves the local transportation system per the Clark County Comprehensive Growth Management
        Plan and is included in the Council-adopted 2026-2031 Transportation Improvement Program.

        On March 18, 2025, Council approved Resolution 2025-03-08 (PW25-068) authorizing closure of Northeast
        179th Street at Northeast 29th Avenue for a period not to exceed 730 days ending before April 30, 2027. The
        approval was granted under the assumption that project construction would begin in June 2025, however, the
        construction contract was not executed until July 21, 2025, and the first road closure was implemented on
        August 25, 2025.

        Staff are seeking approval of this resolution to update the start and completion dates for project and
        associated road closures. Staff are not requesting a change to the original stated duration of the road closures,
        730 days.

        Staff will continue to ensure the Contractor understands the impact this work is having on the community and
        adjacent property owners. The County continues to require the Contractor to ensure access for residents of
        adjacent properties and coordinate with local services providers including emergency services.

        Review by applicable departments/offices:
        The Construction Section will continue to coordinate with the County’s Traffic Engineering and
        Communications Sections regarding information related to current closure configurations and/or changes to
        closures.

        Prior council discussions/direction/approval:
        PW25-068 (SR74-25) and Resolution 2025-03-08 were previously approved and resolved on March 18, 2025.

        COUNCIL POLICY IMPLICATIONS
        There are no Council policy implications resulting from this action. The requested action will not alter details
        presented in the original resolution. All notification regarding closures will follow RCW47.48.020.



        ADMINISTRATIVE POLICY IMPLICATIONS
        There are no administrative policy implications resulting from this action. Specific closure dates and alternate
        route information will be provided to the public in accordance with RCW 47.48.020.



        COMMUNITY OUTREACH
        This project is included in the Council-adopted 2026-2031 Transportation Improvement Program and was
        included in the discussions about lifting urban holding, which Council approved in 2019. A public meeting was
        held on August 12, 2025, prior to start of construction.

        Public Works is required to notify nearby residents and the traveling public via: variable messaging signs
        placed five days prior to start of the closure; mailers to neighbors prior to start of the closure, including a map
        of the detour; information and detour route posted to the County’s website; a news release issued to local
        media via FlashAlert; at least one post on Public Works’s social media profiles and via a resident email
        notification system. Public Works has notified emergency response agencies, service providers and other
        impacted parties as required by the standard County traffic control permit.
