Consistentwith RCW36.40,supplemental budgetappropriationsmay beconsideredthroughoutthefiscal
yearwhencircumstanceswarrant.Supplemental budgetrequests aregenerallyappropriateunderthe
following conditions:
     . An unforeseen circumstance that poses a significant risk to the health, safety, or welfare of dark
        Countyresidents.
    .   An unanticipatedconditionthat materially impairsa department'sor elected official'sabilityto fulfill a
        statutory or legal obligation.
    .   A laboragreement, legal settlement, arbitration award,or courtjudgmentthatexceedsthe
        appropriations available within the adopted budget.
    .   The receiptof unanticipatedrevenuesthataredirectlyassociatedwith newexpenditures,including
        grant awards or other restricted funding sources.
