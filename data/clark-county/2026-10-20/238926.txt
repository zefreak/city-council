        BACKGROUND

        dark County Public Health (CCPH) has prepared dark County Code Chapter 24. 22, Drinking Water
        Regulations. The ordinance is intended to provide a consistent local framework for individual wells, two-party
        shared water systems and small Group B public water systems, while adopting applicable state requirements.
        The ordinance also clarifies the roles and responsibilities of the Health Officer, owners, responsible parties,
        applicants, and operators.

        A major objective of the ordinance is to separate enforceable code requirements from technical and
        administrative implementation details. Core legal requirements remain in Chapter 24.22, while detailed
        design standards, review procedures, forms, standard details, operations and maintenance guidance, and staff
        procedures will be maintained in supporting technical and administrative manuals. This structure is intended
        to make the code easier to use and maintain while allowing technical guidance to be updated as standards
        and practices change.

        Review by applicable departments/offices:
        Community Development Review: dark County Public Health provided colleagues in the Community
        Development Department with the draft code for review and comment.

        Prosecuting Attorney Review: The County's Prosecuting Attorney's Office has been engaged throughout
        development of the ordinance. A final review was completed to prepare for this staff report

        Informal industry review: The ordinancewas developed through coordinated review by CCPhl staff with
        input from local industry professionals, since a technical advisory committee has not yet been initiated. Staff
        used institutional knowledgefrom many years of implementing state drinking water regulations to tailor the
        new code to fill identified gaps and meet local needs.

 96      The dark County Council findsthat the followingrules and regulations are
 97      necessaryto protect and maintain health and sanitarymeasuresgoverning

107      (a) The purpose of these regulations is to protect public health by:
108

110              minimum requirements for individual wells and publicwatersystems and
111              their conveyance infrastructure, includingwaterwells, piping, pumps,

116              managementand operation of individual wells and Group B publicwater
117              systems;

119              of individual wells or watersystems, and the elimination of public health
120              hazards; and
121      (v)     Requiringall public water systems and individual wells to comply with
122              applicablefederal, state, and local drinkingwater requirements.
123
124              These regulations establish requirements for the location, design,
125              installation, operation, maintenance,and monitoringof drinkingwater
126              systems and drinking water infrastructure to:
127
128      (vi)    Ensurethat individualwells, individual well watersystems, publicwater
129              system wells, and public water systems provide a safe and reliable source of
130              potable drinkingwater; and
131
132      (vii)   Minimize potential public health risks associatedwith contaminated water
133              sources, impairment of drinking water resources, and waterborne illness.

135      responsible party or an individual well or Group B publicdrinkingwatersystem
