        BACKGROUND
        A temporary road closure for the Whipple Creek Bridge, Knapps Station Bridge, and Carson Bridge
        maintenance work project (PRJ0001799 / CRP #320322) was approved on June 16, 2026. The closure was
        authorized to begin no earlier than July 5, 2026, and end no later than December 31, 2026, for a duration not
        exceeding 180 days. However, due to extenuating circumstances, the bid opening date has been rescheduled
        to October 6, 2026. As a result, a new temporary road closure request is required.

        This construction requires a work area that will not safely accommodate the proposed work and two-way
        traffic simultaneously. Clark County Public Works understands the impact this work will have on the
        community, adjacent property owners and access for emergency vehicles. To reduce impacts on the
        community, the contractor will provide access for property residents and emergency vehicles.

        Clark County Public Works anticipates the closures starting in December 2026.

        Review by applicable departments/offices:
        This resolution has been reviewed by the Transportation Division, the County Engineer, and Legal.

        COUNCIL POLICY IMPLICATIONS
        There are no Council policy implications resulting from this action. This agreement supports the Clark County
        Council’s practice of investing in the County road system by providing safe and efficient transportation
        systems for all County residents.



        ADMINISTRATIVE POLICY IMPLICATIONS
        There are no administrative policy implications resulting from this action. Specific closure dates and alternate
        route information will be provided to the public in accordance with RCW 47.48.020.



        COMMUNITY OUTREACH
        The contractor will post variable message signs five days prior to the start of the closure. The contractor will
        notify nearby residents before the start of construction. The contractor will issue a news release and post
        project and closure information on its website, including a map of the detour route, prior to the start of the
        road closure. The contractor will also post this information on their social media profiles; if the contractor does
        not have social media profiles, they will notify Public Works staff. Public Works will share the contractor’s
        social posts related to this proposed closure or, if the contractor does not have social media profiles, will post
        about the closure. The contractor will notify emergency response agencies and coordinate with other
        impacted parties as needed as required by the standard county traffic control permit.

1           WHEREAS, the Clark County Council is the legislative body charged by state law under

2    RCW 47.48.010 and WAC 308-330-270 with authority to close county roads; and,

3           WHEREAS, Clark County Public Works intends to hire a contractor to perform bridge

4      strengthening maintenance work on Whipple Creek Bridge, Knapps Station Bridge, and Carson

5      Bridge as part of project PRJ#0001799; and,

6           WHEREAS, the proposed work will require excavation within all lanes of travel; and,

7           WHEREAS, there is not room to safely accommodate the proposed work and two-way

8    traffic simultaneously; and,

9           WHEREAS, bridge strength will be reduced during construction due to the maintenance

10   work; and,

11           WHEREAS, vibrations from traffic would disrupt bridge maintenance work; and,

12          WHEREAS, the county engineer believes the safety of contractors and the traveling

13   public will be best served by temporary closures of Northwest 179th Street between Northwest

14   51st Avenue and Northwest 41st Avenue, Northwest Krieger Road between Northwest 179th

15   Street and Northwest 192nd Street, and Northeast 67th Avenue between Northeast 209th

16   Street and Northeast 219th Street; and,

17          WHEREAS, the contractor has up to 180 calendar days to complete all bridge

18   maintenance work, anticipated to start in December 2026 or January 2027; and,

19          WHEREAS, each individual roadway closure shall be limited to a maximum duration of

20   20 working days; and,

21          WHEREAS, Northwest 179th Street and Northwest Krieger Road shall not be closed at the

22   same time; and,

23          WHEREAS, the contractor will provide timely notification to the public, by publication in

24   The Columbian, of an established detour route, and posting notifications at the termini of the

25   proposed closures; and,
