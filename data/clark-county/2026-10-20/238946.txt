        BACKGROUND
        In 2024 Council approved a contract with Columbia West Engineering Inc. for construction material testing
        services to support capital project delivery. The original contract terms set an annual not to exceed spend limit
        of $225,000 annually and up to $1,125,000 total over the maximum life of the contract. Due to fluctuations in
        market prices for services and project construction timing, the needs for materials testing services have
        exceeded the set annual limit of $225,000 in the current year and are forecasted to exceed the limit for
        upcoming years. To effectively manage materials testing services going forward, staff recommend and request
        modifying the contract language to eliminate the annual spending limit to allow increased flexibility in
        managing the necessary services each year for the remainder of the contract.

        The recommended change does not change the previously approved and budgeted total maximum not to
        exceed contract amount of $1,125,000.
