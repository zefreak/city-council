Purpose of Today’s Work Session
• Review the Clark County Code sections affected by the
  transition of Solid Waste Operations and Solid Waste
  Education and Outreach to Public Works
  o Compost procurement and reporting updates (CCC 2.37.030–
    2.37.040)
  o Compost education and procurement reporting changes (CCC
    24.12.370–24.12.380)
  o $1.43 tip fee to pay for Public Health solid waste services (CCC
    24.12.130)
• Confirm next steps and Clark County Council direction
Compost Procurement Requirements
• RCW 43.19A.150 requires counties to
  adopt compost procurement measures.

• Council adopted the compost
  procurement ordinance in 2023
  (SR2023-1828).

• Since adoption, both the Clark County
  organizational structure and Washington
  State Department of Ecology’s (Ecology)
  reporting requirements have changed,
  prompting updates to local code.
CCC 2.37.030 through 2.37.040 - revise
• Aligns with the current
  organizational structure
• Shifts reporting responsibilities
  from Public Health to Solid Waste
  and Recycling
• Updates reporting deadlines to
  match Ecology’s timeline
• Clarifies conditions for exemptions when delays occur
• Completion of these code updates is required before finalizing
  the countywide policy as required by RCW.
CCC 24.12.370 through 24.12.380 - rescind
• These sections currently sit in Public Health’s chapter of Clark
  County Code, outlining compost-related education and
  additional reporting details.
• Ecology has confirmed that our Comprehensive Solid Waste
  Management Plan already meets the intent of the RCW
  requirements.
• These sections are proposed for removal to reflect the
  organizational shift and eliminate redundancy.
Addition of the Temporary $1.43 Tip Fee
• In 2017, council adopted a $1.43 per-ton
  fee to support Public Health’s solid and
  hazardous waste work during a pause in
  state funding.

• This tip fee was added as an addition to
  CCC 24.14.130 Fees.

• Since then, state funding has been
  restored, and solid waste responsibilities
  have been reorganized within Clark
  County.
CCC 24.12.130 – rescind 2017 addition
• Public Health and Public Works
  evaluated the fee and determined it is no
  longer warranted.

• This section of CCC.24.130 is proposed
  to be rescinded.
Requested Action
• Seeking council approval to move forward with a public hearing
  for the proposed ordinance, to be effective January 1, 2027.
• Tentative schedule for public hearing set for 6 pm Tuesday, Oct.
  20.
