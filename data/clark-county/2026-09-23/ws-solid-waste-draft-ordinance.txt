 3   AN ORDINANCE amending Clark County Code related to solid waste and recycling, including
 4   sections CCC 2.37.030 through 2.37.040, CCC 24.12.370 through 24.12.380, and CCC 24.12.130,
 5   to align the code with current organizational structure, state regulatory requirements, and program
 6   administration.
 7          WHEREAS, maintaining the accuracy, consistency, and usability of the Clark County
 8   Code is essential to ensure that solid waste requirements remain clear, enforceable, and aligned
 9   with current state law and administrative practice; and
10          WHEREAS, in March 2025, Solid Waste Operations and Solid Waste Education and
11   Outreach programs were formally transferred from the Public Health Department to the Public
12   Works Department, necessitating updates to Clark County Code to reflect current departmental
13   responsibilities and program oversight; and
14          WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 2017-09-10, later codified in CCC 24.12.130, established a
15   temporary $1.43 per-ton solid waste tipping fee to support essential solid and hazardous waste
16   assessment, response, and mitigation services, and this fee is no longer necessary under the revised
17   administrative structure mutually established by Public Health and Public Works following the
18   2025 transfer of solid waste functions; and
19          WHEREAS, in 2022 the Washington State Legislature enacted Engrossed Second
20   Substitute House Bill (ESSHB) 1799, codified in Chapter 43.19A RCW, requiring counties to
21   adopt compost procurement measures, and Clark County subsequently adopted the required
22   procurement, education, outreach, and reporting provisions in 2023; and updates by the
23   Washington State Department of Ecology to statewide reporting timelines, combined with
24   Ecology’s authorization to address compost education and reporting requirements through the
25   local solid waste management plan, necessitate revision of CCC 2.37.030 and the rescission of
26   corresponding sections of CCC 24.12; and
27          WHEREAS, the Council is considering these matters at a duly advertised public hearing
28   and finds that adoption of this ordinance will further the public health, safety, and welfare; now,
29   therefore,

 4          Section 1. Amendatory. Sec. 13 of Ord. 1978-02-01 as most recently amended by Sec.
 5   1 of Ord. 2017-09-10 and codified as CCC 24.12.130 are each amended to read:
 6          24.12.130 Fees.
 7          The jurisdictional board of health may establish reasonable fees for permits and renewal
 8          of permits. All permit fees collected by the jurisdictional board of health shall be
 9          deposited in the county treasury and to the account from which Clark County public
10          health’s operating expenses are paid. In addition to such application fee, any permit
11          issued hereunder shall be conditioned upon payment of a reasonable operations fee set by
12          the jurisdictional board of health upon the recommendation of the solid waste advisory
13          commission in an amount sufficient to cover the estimated costs to the county and the
14          jurisdictional board of health in monitoring compliance with the requirements of this
15          chapter and the permit. The county and the jurisdictional board of health shall by
16          interlocal agreement provide for the distribution of the application and operation fees in
17          accordance with the services provided by each.

25          Section 2.37.030 Compost Procurement Policy
26          General Policy. The County shall develop and maintain a procurement policy and plan for
27          the purchase and use of compost materials.

29                    whether compost can be utilized in a county project as required in
30                    RCW 43.19A.120. In the event that a department identifies that compost can be
31                    utilized, compost shall be specified for use on the project.
