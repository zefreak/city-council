BACKGROUND

Background:
Clark County has historically maintained both a Health Officer and a Deputy Health Officer to ensure
continuity of public health leadership and adequate coverage during periods of absence. With the recent
retirement of the Health Officer and the appointment of an Interim Health Officer, the department has
continued its commitment to maintaining stable and compliant public health oversight. The Interim Health
Officer has a planned vacation, and additional coverage is needed to ensure uninterrupted fulfillment of
statutory responsibilities, timely decision-making, and ongoing support to Public Health Officer countywide
duties during her absence.
