Re:     Letter of Concurrence – NW 31st Ave Safety Improvements (HSIP)

To Whom It May Concern:

The Cowlitz Indian Tribe (CIT) has proposed safety improvements along approximately 0.30 miles of NW 31st
Ave, a segment that spans both tribal jurisdiction and unincorporated Clark County right-of-way (ROW). The
scope includes a blend of Federal Highway Administration (FHWA)-approved safety countermeasures
including shoulder widening, rumble strips, high-visibility thermoplastic, reflective raised pavement markers
(RPMs), signage, illumination, and pavement repair/resurfacing.

Clark County understands that the Washington State Department of Transportation (WSDOT) intends to
award approximately $750,000 in Highway Safety Improvement Program (HSIP) funding contingent upon
receipt of county concurrence. Clark County supports the proposed project and provides its concurrence,
conditioned upon execution of an Interlocal Agreement between the Cowlitz Indian Tribe and Clark County
prior to any work within County ROW.

Clark County appreciates the Tribe’s commitment to improving safety for our shared community and we look
forward to partnering to deliver these improvements.
