Policy Updates
Presented by Jordan Boege, Senior Policy Analyst
    • Legislative Request: Mobile Home Parks - Rent Cap Laws
           o Jordan provided an overview of HB 1217, which implemented a rent increase cap of 5% for mobile
               home park unit rents annually. That limit does not apply when a resident moves out. Councilor Yung
               requested this topic as a potential legislative advocacy effort for the county.
           o Yung stated he envisioned an amendment to current law that would remove mobile home parks from
               the carve out to increase rent beyond the 5% cap.
           o Council supported having this item come back with more detail. Jordan stated he would work with
               Councilor Yung on drafting a proposal to bring back to Council.
