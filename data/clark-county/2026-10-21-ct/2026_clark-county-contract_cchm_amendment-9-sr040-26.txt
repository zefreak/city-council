                BACKGROUND

                Back round:
                Clark County and the Clark County Historical Museum entered into a contract on August 9, 2016,
                for in-kind and monetary support services to begin in the 2017 -18 biennium. This contract is
                amended on an annual basis to allow the council to continue the annual in-kind and monetary
                support services. The monetary support for 2026 in the amount of $60,000 is covered by a one-
                dollar surcharge that is collected per RCW 36.22.170. The dollar surcharge shall be used at the
                discretion of the county council to promote historical preservation or historical programs, which may
                include preservation of historic documents.

                In addition to the monetary support the county will provide the following in-kind services to the
                museum:
                         a. 2,500 sq. ft. for collection storage in the Pepsi building.
                         b. Printing services; said services tci be provided up to an estimated maximum of $10,000
                             for the year 2026.
                         c. Provide publicity for museum events in the county employee newsletter, FYI.
                         d. Routine Vehicle maintenance including oil change and inspections for one Historical
                             Museum Van. Maintenance can be performed by fleet at current rates. Secure outside
                             parking at 78th Street Operation Center for one vehicle

                    WHEREAS, CCHM will be provided by the County all the required services as outlined in
            the original agreement for the budget year of 2026 and;

                  \VHEREAS, the original agreement allowed for changes in the scope of services when
