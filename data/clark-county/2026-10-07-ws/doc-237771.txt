2027 Budget Submission at a Glance
2027 Budget Environment
Major factors affecting the Internal Services department's 2027 budget
include staffing levels, service demand from our internal customers,
aging infrastructure, unfunded regulatory requirements, contractual
increase, and cost increase of services, utilities and supplies.
Primary Budget Focus for 2027
The department request focus on keeping up with workload demands,
inflationary increases, and contractual cost.
Key Considerations for Council
• Space Planning efforts continue as we look to modernize existing
  buildings including the Jail, Courthouse, and Sheriff facilities.
• Addressing staffing/workload capacity -Purchasing, Finance, and
  Project Facility Positions
• Contractual Increases-Security and Custodial Services
