2027 Annual Budget

Body

Clark County’s budget process follows state requirements specified by RCW 36.40.   The council serves as the county’s legislative authority, implementing policy by allocating funding to county departments and elected offices.  The county manager takes policy directions from the council and coordinates with the Budget Office, which is responsible for facilitating, preparing, and updating the county budget.  The county manager recommends a balanced budget to the council that fulfils their policy direction and is within the resources available to the county. 

Key Dates for the 2027 Annual Budget Adoption:
  • Department/Office Requests Published: October 5, 2026 
  • Departments/Elected Offices Budget Work Session: October 7, 2026
  • County Manager Recommended Budget Published: November 3, 2026
  • County Manager’s Recommended Budget Work Session: November 4, 2026
  • Budget Hearing (Elected Official’s Testimony): November 30, 2026
  • Budget Hearing (Public Testimony): December 1, 2026
  • Budget Hearing continuation, if needed: December 2, 2026

2027 Department Submissions Budget Reports - October 5, 2026

  • 2027 Department Submissions Stage Reports - October 5, 2026*
  • 2027 Department/Elected Offices Work Session - October 7, 2026
    • Council Work Session Recording

*This publication represents the first of three key stages in the development and adoption of Clark County’s 2027 Annual Budget. It presents the budget information and change requests submitted by County departments and elected offices for consideration and provides an initial view of proposed changes to the County’s 2027 operating and capital budgets.

The three primary stages of the annual budget process are the Department Submission, County Manager Recommended, and Adopted Budget stages. Information presented at the Department Submission stage is preliminary and remains subject to review, revision, recommendation, and final action as the budget process progresses.

The 2027 Department Submission Budget Reports include:

  • Operating Revenues by Fund Report
  • Operating Expenditures by Fund and Expenditures by Function and Fund Reports
  • Capital Revenues by Fund Report
  • Capital Expenditures by Fund and Expenditures by Function and Fund Reports
  • Exhibit B: Change Request Narratives with Summary by Fund Report, which presents new funding requests submitted by departments and elected offices for consideration during the 2027 budget process. This report identifies the operating and capital budget impacts associated with each request.

Important Information About Ballot Measures and Budget Scenarios: The Department Submission Budget Reports include certain change requests developed for financial and operational planning purposes. These requests allow the County to evaluate potential budget impacts under different circumstances and should not be interpreted as County Manager recommendations or final budget decisions.

Ballot Measure Financial Impacts: The Budget Office is currently finalizing the change request packages reflecting the financial and operational impacts associated with voter approval of Clark County Proposition No. 12 – Bonds for Public Safety and Criminal Justice Capital Infrastructure and Clark County Proposition No. 13 – Levy Lid Lift for Public Safety and Criminal Justice Services. These packages will identify the related budgetary impacts and funding strategies and will be formally incorporated into the next round of budget reports as part of the County Manager Recommended stage.

Alternate Scenarios – 70 Series: Change requests numbered in the “70” series identify alternative funding and operational priorities submitted by departments and offices for consideration if Proposition No. 12 and/or Proposition No. 13 are not approved by voters. These requests are intended to support budget planning by identifying departmental priorities and potential funding needs under an alternative ballot measure outcome. Inclusion of a 70-series request in the Department Submission reports represents a planning alternative only and does not indicate that the request has been recommended or approved for funding.

General Fund Reduction Scenarios – 90 Series: Change requests numbered in the “90” series represent potential General Fund expenditure reductions submitted as part of the County’s financial planning process. These scenarios identify potential reductions and their associated operational or service impacts should General Fund reductions become necessary. Inclusion of a 90-series request in the Department Submission reports does not indicate that the reduction has been recommended or approved.

Report Timing and Subsequent Submissions: The Department Submission Budget Reports reflect change requests received and processed for this stage of the 2027 budget process. Additional requests submitted after the Department Submission reporting deadline, as well as revisions to existing requests, may be incorporated into subsequent stages of the budget process and therefore may not appear in these reports.

All change requests presented in the Department Submission Budget Reports remain subject to further review and consideration. The County Manager’s recommendations and final decisions of the Clark County Council may differ from the requests, assumptions, and planning scenarios presented at this stage.

Next Steps in the Budget Process:  The next publication will be the 2027 Recommended Budget, scheduled for release on November 3, 2026. This publication will reflect the County Manager’s recommendations to the Clark County Council and will incorporate additional information and revisions developed following the Department Submission stage.

The final publication will be the 2027 Adopted Budget, reflecting the budget approved by the Clark County Council following the public hearing process beginning November 30, 2026.
