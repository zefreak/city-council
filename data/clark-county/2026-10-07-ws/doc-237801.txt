2027 Budget Submission at a Glance
2027 Budget Environment

Human Resources budget is driven by rising liability costs, increasing insurance premiums, and higher claims and
litigation pressures across the organization. Additionally, growing regulatory requirements, staffing capacity needs, and
expanded workload in safety oversight, emergency management, and compensation equity are contributing to increased
service demand and resource needs.

Primary Budget Focus for 2027

The primary goal of these budget requests is to ensure the County’s risk, safety, emergency management, and
compensation systems have the resources needed to remain compliant, financially stable, and able to meet growing
organizational demands.

Key Considerations for Council

• Significant increases in liability claims, legal fees and insurance premiums

• Rising workload and compliance obligations in county-wide emergency management and safety programs

• Increased expenses projected for the Self-Insurance fund

• Need for strategic investment in compensation equity for non-represented employees

• Infrastructure assessment requirements for county-owned railroad bridges and trestles

• A 2.5% reduction to the HR budget totals $52,255, taken from the controllable budget; this reduction would limit the
  department’s ability to provide essential staff training in coming years among other items.
