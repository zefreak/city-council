Road Fund 1012: Pressures and Actions
2027 Budget Environment​
•   Structural pressure: Operating costs outpacing recurring revenues​
•   Result: Road Fund available for capital diminishes each year


Key Considerations for Council
•   Even with a 1% levy action, the TIP is projected to dip into Road
    Fund reserves by the end of the 6-year period
•   Result: Jeopardized grant leverage, reduced ability to meet capital
    commitments, future discussions regarding level-of-service
    reductions
•   Action: Direction from Council to explore alternate revenue
    opportunities

Public Works Department
2027 Budget Priority Summary
1% Levy Increases
   Impact: $525,725 Road | $44,963 GCPD | $34,848 Conservation Futures
   Council consideration: Without the increase each fund faces service reductions, weakened capital and
   grant-matching capacity. Adoption of the 1% levy preserves core services against rising costs.


PWK-05-27AD County Parks Regional Maintenance & Ops Subsidy Increase
   Request: $390,661 | General Fund 0001 one-time transfer
   Council consideration: Parks cannot maintain current level of service for operations without this. Maintain
   open parks while in transition to stability.

PWK-25-26FL- Regional Funding Market Research Request
   Request: $220,000 | MPD Operations Fund 1032
   Council consideration: Without research, the county will be unable to prepare for a public measure. Acquire
   essential data for regional funding measure.
