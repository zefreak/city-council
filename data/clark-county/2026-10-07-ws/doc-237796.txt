2027 Priority Items for Council Consideration
PRIORITY 1 — PUD-70-27AD Public Defense Increase in Criminal Defense Contracts: $950,000 General Fund/Ongoing

Why it matters: This is to retain our current public defense contracts and attract new contracts with a rate of pay that is in
line with the market.

Council consideration: Our Clark County public defense contractors are so dedicated to our community and providing this
constitutional right to those Clark County citizens that qualify. This is an integral part of the Clark County Criminal Justice
System.
PRIORITY 2 — PUD-73-27AD Public Defense Four New Investigator Positions: $454,000 | 4 FTE | General Fund/Ongoing
$383,012

Why it matters: When we did the financial model, bringing as many investigators in-house is the most cost-effective option
due to the high hourly rate of more experienced investigators and the addition of sales tax to all investigation services as of
Oct 1, 2025. We currently contract out 100%. This is a mandatory function of a public defense office needed to investigate
cases. This work would default to our public defenders if not contracted by investigators.

Council consideration: This is a cost we are already incurring, but since 2025 we are also paying sales tax on this cost.
Bringing in house could make this request budget neutral.
PRIORITY 3 — PUD-90-27AD Public Defense Public Defense 2.5% Reduction Scenario: -$220,559

Why it matters: With the addition of Investigator FTEs (above), we anticipate our investigative charges in the services
ledger to decrease and would like to present that as our permanent $220,559 decrease to meet the 2.5% budget reduction
scenario.

Council consideration: If approved, we may have to go to council for emergency funds in the fall supplemental in 2027 as
the number of investigative hours per case can and has fluctuated based on how many cases we get in a year and the
severity of those cases.
