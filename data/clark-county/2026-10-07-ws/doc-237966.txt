2027 Budget Submission at a Glance
2027 Budget Environment
The Clerk’s Office must establish a functional management structure. Without budget
support, the office will operate six distinct units with 49 employees all reporting to a single
manager.
Primary Budget Focus for 2027
The Clerk’s Office must address its current management structure. The current structure is
impractical, diminishes operational effectiveness, strains employee support, and exposes
the county to substantial organizational and legal risk. Addressing this structural deficiency
is the Clerk’s Office’s highest budget priority.
Key Considerations for Council
• Adequate management is essential to train staff, oversee daily operations, and ensure the
  Clerk’s Office can meet its mandated duties and serve over 30,000 in-office customers
  each year
• Growing workloads and increasing complexity require sufficient supervisory capacity to
  maintain compliance and quality
• Accurate records are a core statutory obligation which depend on consistent auditing,
  performance monitoring, and timely oversight
• Without these additional positions the county is exposing itself to unnecessary risk and
  liability, is unable to adequately train and mentor staff, and will see increased employee
  fatigue and turnover across the office.

2027 Budget Environment

Human Resources budget is driven by rising liability costs, increasing insurance premiums, and higher claims and
litigation pressures across the organization. Additionally, growing regulatory requirements, staffing capacity needs, and

organizational demands.

• Rising workload and compliance obligations in county-wide emergency management and safety programs

• Increased expenses projected for the Self-Insurance fund

• Infrastructure assessment requirements for county-owned railroad bridges and trestles

• A 2.5% reduction to the HR budget totals $52,255, taken from the controllable budget; this reduction would limit the
  department’s ability to provide essential staff training in coming years among other items.

2027 Budget Submission at a Glance
2027 Budget Environment
Local jails are the hub of the law and justice system.
Carceral facilities in Washington are required to maintain employee certification
standards and a provide a basic level of security and care to incarcerated persons.
These requirements continue to increase costs for employees, incarcerated persons
and the physical jail facilities.
In the last few years, Clark County has made significant investments in the jail.
Jail Services has applied for and received multiple alternative funding streams to
increase staffing, build a 64-bed jail facility, increase jail beds by reopening previously
closed housing areas and increased/improve in-custody programs.
If Propositions 12 and 13 pass in the November election, several budget requests will
be unnecessary.


Primary Budget Focus for 2027
Jail Services focused 2027 budget requests on on-going employee needs
and the opening of J-Pod, a new 64-bed medium security jail facility.
