2027 Budget Submission at a Glance
2027 Budget Environment
The Clerk’s Office must establish a functional management structure. Without budget
support, the office will operate six distinct units with 49 employees all reporting to a single
manager.
Primary Budget Focus for 2027
The Clerk’s Office must address its current management structure. The current structure is
impractical, diminishes operational effectiveness, strains employee support, and exposes
the county to substantial organizational and legal risk. Addressing this structural deficiency
is the Clerk’s Office’s highest budget priority.
Key Considerations for Council
• Adequate management is essential to train staff, oversee daily operations, and ensure the
  Clerk’s Office can meet its mandated duties and serve over 30,000 in-office customers
  each year
• Growing workloads and increasing complexity require sufficient supervisory capacity to
  maintain compliance and quality
• Accurate records are a core statutory obligation which depend on consistent auditing,
  performance monitoring, and timely oversight
• Without these additional positions the county is exposing itself to unnecessary risk and
  liability, is unable to adequately train and mentor staff, and will see increased employee
  fatigue and turnover across the office.

Historical Background for Requests
2024 Budget
Recognizing fundamental changes in human resource management, the Clerk realized
the problems of a flat organization and requested three management positions in the
2024 budget, none of which were approved:
• Judicial Operations Manager
• Court Records Manager
• Finance/Accounting Manager
2025 Budget
The Clerk requested one full-time Court Records Manager position. The Council
approved a two-year project position expiring December 31, 2026.
The project position was filled in January 2025 with a highly qualified candidate. In May
2026, the incumbent chose to apply for and accept a permanent full-time position at a
lower classification due to the uncertainty of their future employment.
2026 Budget
No manager was requested due to budget constraints, and the project manager was still
in position.

Current Program Manager Range of Duties

Currently the single Program Manager II carries the full
management load (the list is not inclusive of all duties):
• All leave approvals and payroll for staff of 47 (49 in 2027)
• Oversight of all customer service issues
• Grant management
• Accounts payable.
• Budget
• All employee HR issues including performance and disciplinary matters
• All new employee onboarding
• Oversight of all training
• Participation in several interdepartmental and inter-organizational committees
• Ensuring compliance with all statutory guidelines and requirements

2027 Priority Items for Council Consideration
PRIORITY 1 — Convert Court Records Manager from Project to Regular/ #CLK-03-27AD
Request: $114,857| 1.0 FTE | General Fund/Ongoing

Why it matters: The Clerk is the custodian of the permanent record of Superior Court, receiving over
400,000 documents per year for filing and responsible for the processing, tracking, indexing, and
maintenance of over 12 million permanent records.

Council consideration: Without this critical management position, the Clerks Office cannot provide the
oversight needed for this critical task and with the leadership, training and auditing, and management of
the Court Records unit.


PRIORITY 2 — 1.0 FTE Program Manager I – Judicial Operations/ #CLK-04-27AD
Request: $135,588| 1.0 FTE | General Fund/Ongoing

Why it matters: The in-court unit of 21 (in 2027) is a critical and specialized team meeting the mandated
duty of being present at all Superior Court proceedings (approximately 49,000 in 2025) and it warrants
the leadership of a manager focused on scheduling, training, auditing, mentoring, and assistance.
Council consideration: Without this management position, the Clerk’s Office cannot provide the training
and oversight this critical unit requires, resulting in diminished work quality and reduced service to the
Court and the community.
