2027 Priority Items for Council Consideration
PRIORITY 1 — Funding for Position P003024 (GIS-01-27AD)
REQUEST: $37,002 in ongoing funding from the General Fund
• Per MOU #2022-GIS-01 between Clark County and the County Assessor, this position is
  required to be funded at 100% FTE (currently at 60%).
• This position processes over half of the property ownership transfers that the county
  receives.
• This position also maintains property information used for calculating property taxes and
  for providing property information to other downstream, core county functions.
• Risk if not approved: non-compliance with MOU, delays in property transfer processing
  will disrupt core county services for residents and businesses, and property data on the
  MapsOnline and PIC websites will become outdated.


PRIORITY 2 — GIS Revenue Adjustments (GIS-02-27AD)
REQUEST: Reduce 2027 revenue forecast by $39,000
• Risk if not approved: 2027 revenue forecast will be overestimated by $39,000.

2027 Priority Items for Council Consideration
PRIORITY 3 — GIS 2.5% General Fund Budget Reduction (GIS-90-27AD)
Impact: $85,168 or $45,032
• $85,168 = 2.5% of the entire 2027 GIS budget.
• $45,032 = 2.5% of the General Fund contribution to the 2027 GIS budget.
• Option 1: Eliminate position #P003024
    Impact: $51,885
    Consequences: non-compliance with MOU, growing property transfer backlogs, outdated
     property data on MapsOnline and PIC, and processing delays that disrupt core county
     services for businesses and residents.
• Option 2: Cut up to 60% from remaining portion of controllable budget
    Impact: $33,283
    Consequences: degraded service levels, diminished revenue generation, weakened support
     for core county systems, and heightened risk of failing to meet statutory mandates.
• Recommendation: Reject this budget reduction package to avoid service disruptions,
  preserve our ability to generate revenue and pay for operational needs, and ensure GIS
  has adequate capacity to support departments with implementing technology-based
  solutions that offset their budget reductions.
