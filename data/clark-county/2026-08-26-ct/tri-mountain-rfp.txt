The County’s Goals with the RFP include:
• Having all course management, operations, and maintenance
  activities performed professionally and with minimal County
  operational support;
• Preserving the physical condition of the courses as a valuable
  County-owned asset;
• Providing golf facilities and amenities that are an improvement
  over current facilities and amenities; and
• Providing a quality, family friendly experience at affordable
  rates for the citizens of the Clark County.
• Maximizing the return on its assets, and minimizing the
  County’s financial risk associated with the golf course;

Term of Lease
A contract awarded as a result of this RFP will be for an initial period
of ten (10) years and is intended to begin on January 1, 2027 and end
December 31, 2036.
Total lease terms and payments to the County, including extensions,
will be negotiated determined by evaluating the selected proposal(s)
Clark County reserves the right to extend the lease resulting from this
RFP for a period of four (4) additional terms, in ten (10) year
increments, with the same terms and conditions as the initial term or a
change in terms and conditions mutual agreed upon by both parties,
by service of a written notice of its intention to do so prior to the
contract termination date. Payment to the County for additional option
term(s) shall be reviewed prior to extension of the contract.
The county also reserves the right to terminate the contract, with
ninety (90) days written notice, at any time if the requirements of the
lease are not being met satisfactorily, solely in the county’s judgment.
