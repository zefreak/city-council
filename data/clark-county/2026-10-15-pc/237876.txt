Purpose

• Title 36.70A RCW (GMA) requires a particular process in
  order to amend Clark County’s Comprehensive Plan.

• Clark County Code (CCC) 40.560.040.B states as follows:
  “All amendments to the comprehensive plan are legislative
  actions subject to a Type IV process (Section 40.510.040).”
  CCC 40.560.040.M, lists a variety of plan amendment
  categories, including TIP amendments, that therefore fall into
  the Type IV review process.
