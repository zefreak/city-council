                                        DETERMINATION OF NONSIGNIFICANCE (DNS)

                 Description of Proposal:
                 Clark County is proposing to adopt an update to its existing Parks and Lands Capital
                 Improvement Plan (CIP) for years 2027 through 2032, as a non-project SEPA review, as
                 required by law, to reflect changes to the overall program. The Parks and Lands CIP
                 identifies park and legacy land acquisition and improvement project phases to be completed
                 during the next six-year period within unincorporated Clark County. The purpose of this
                 program is to prepare a comprehensive park, open space and conservation improvement
                 work schedule that addresses resident needs and financial constraints while considering
                 environmental concerns.

                 Proponent: Clark County Public Works

                 Location of proposal, including street address, if any:
                 Various locations throughout Clark County

                 Lead Agency: Clark County Public Works, the lead agency for this proposal, has
                 determined that it does not have a probable significant adverse impact on the environment.
                 An environmental impact statement (EIS) is not required under RCW 43.21C.030(2)(c). This
                 decision was made after review of the attached environmental checklist and other
                 information on file with the lead agency. This information is available to the public on
                 request. The Environmental Checklist is also available at:
                 https://clark.wa.gov/public-works/sepa.

                 This determination is based on the following findings and conclusions:
                 The lead agency has determined that the requirements for environmental analysis,
                 protection, and mitigation measures have been adequately addressed in the development
                 regulations and comprehensive plan adopted under chapter 36.70A RCW, and in other
                 applicable local, state, or federal laws or rules, as provided by RCW 43.21C.240 and WAC
                 197-11-158. This is a non-project action and as such, there are no impacts to the
                 environment due to this action. Each project in the Parks and Lands CIP will go through a
                 specific environmental review during design, avoiding and minimizing impacts as practicable
                 and mitigating any resulting impacts.

                 This DNS is issued under WAC 197-11-340(2); the lead agency will not act on this proposal
                 for 15 days from the date of the notice.
