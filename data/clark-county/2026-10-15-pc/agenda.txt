                1. CPZ-2026-00001 Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area Code Update. The Columbia
                   River Gorge Commission recently adopted a Plan Amendment to the Management Plan for
                   the CRGNSA to clarify and address landowners’ needs for disaster recovery. In response,
                   Clark County must adopt amendments to the applicable land use ordinances. The
                   amendments are intended to streamline permitting for landowners in the Gorge rebuilding
                   structures after a disaster.
                   Staff Contact: Michael Sallis, Planner III
                   Michael.Sallis@clark.wa.gov or (564)397-4544

                2. CPZ-2026-00002 2027-2032 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP). The TIP
                   identifies public works capital projects including new construction, reconstruction, bridges,
                   bicycle and pedestrian facilities, safety improvements, asphalt and concrete preservation,
                   and signalization construction to maintain and enhance Clark County’s transportation
                   system in unincorporated Clark County.
                   Staff Contact: Chris Carle, Capital Programming Manager
                   chris.carle@clark.wa.gov or (564)397-4523

                3. CPZ-2026-00003 2027-2032 Stormwater Capital Plan. The Stormwater Capital Plan
                   identifies stormwater capital improvement projects as an important part of Clark County’s
                   Stormwater Management Program to protect water resources from polluted stormwater
                   runoff and comply with state and federal clean water regulations. Capital projects represent
                   a significant investment of Clean Water Fund revenues and help address well-documented
                   impacts of stormwater runoff on local waterways.
                   Staff Contact: Marlena Milosevich Butler, Stormwater Capital Program Manager
                   Marlena.Milosevich@clark.wa.gov or (564)397-4282

 Meeting Details
 Planning Commission meetings are held in a hybrid format and the public is encouraged to participate in the
 following ways:


     1. Attend in person in the 6th floor hearing room, Public Service Center, 1300 Franklin St, Vancouver. If
        providing testimony, follow directions provided by staff and speak during the public comment portion
        of the hearing. At the hearing, you may also submit written comments, maps, photos, graphics, or
        other relevant evidence, for the record.

     2. Attend virtually via Webex (by phone or computer/mobile device). If providing spoken testimony,
        follow directions provided by staff and speak during the public comment portion of the hearing. The
        Webex link, phone number, and details on how to provide testimony via Webex will be posted to the
        Planning Commission meetings webpage at least one week prior to the meeting at
        http://www.clark.wa.gov/pc

     3. Submit written comments to: Jeffrey.delapena@clark.wa.gov or via US Postal Service to the Clark
        County Planning Commission, c/o Jeffrey Delapena, PO Box 9810, Vancouver, WA 98666-9810.
        Written comments should include full name, address, city, zip code, and phone number to be included
        as parties of record. Written comments may be submitted beginning on September 30, 2026, at 8
        a.m. and must be received by the end of the hearing on October 15, 2026.

     4. You may submit items like maps, photos, graphics, articles, legal history, etc., to become part of the
        record of the county’s decision. To do so, you must provide the item. Hand it to staff at the meeting,
        attach it to your digital testimony, or physically send it via USPS or a delivery service that will get it to
        the county before the meeting. Referring to an item, sending a link to an item, or showing an item
        during testimony without giving it to staff, for example, would not enter an item into the record.

     5. If you prefer to watch and listen only: Livestream from your personal computer at www.cvtv.org or
        broadcast cable channel 23.

 The staff report, related materials, hearing agenda, and detailed instructions on how to provide testimony will
 be available 15 days prior to the hearing date on the county’s webpage at http://www.clark.wa.gov/pc

 If you are unable to access these documents, please contact the clerk of the commission, Jeffrey Delapena, at
 jeffrey.delapena@clark.wa.gov or 564.397.4558.
