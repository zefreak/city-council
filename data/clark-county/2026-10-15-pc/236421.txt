15   B.   Resource and Treaty Rights Protections Guidelines.

16     Proposed development reviewed using the expedited review process shall comply with the following resource
17   protection guidelines:

18        1.   Scenic Resources.

19             a. In the GMA, the scenic resource protection guidelines shall not apply to woven-wire fences for
20             agricultural use that would enclose eighty (80) acres or less.

21             b. Except for signs, the colors of structures topographically visible from key viewing areas shall be dark
22             earth tones found at the specific site or the surrounding landscape. The specific colors approved by the
23             responsible official shall be included as a condition of approval. This guideline shall not apply to additions
24             to existing buildings smaller in total area than the existing building, which may be the same color as the
25             existing building.

26             c. Except for signs, structures topographically visible from key viewing areas shall use low or
27             nonreflective building materials, including roofing, gutters, vents, and chimneys.

28             d. Any exterior lighting shall be directed downward and sited, limited in intensity, hooded, and shielded
29             in a manner that prevents lights from being highly visible from key viewing areas and from noticeably
30             contrasting with the surrounding landscape setting, except for road lighting necessary for safety purposes.
31             Shielding and hooding materials shall be composed of nonreflective, opaque materials.

32             e.   Signs shall comply with Section 40.240.300.

33             f. Structures within one-half (1/2) mile of a key viewing area and topographically visible from the key
34             viewing area shall be sited, screened and designed to achieve the applicable scenic standard (e.g., visual
35             subordinance, not visually evident).

36        2.   Cultural Resources.

37             a. The expedited development review process shall only be used to review proposed development that
38             does not require a reconnaissance survey or historic survey, pursuant to Sections 40.240.820(A)(3) and
39             (4).


                                                                3
1              b. The GMA guidelines that protect cultural resources and human remains discovered during
2              construction (Sections 40.240.820(F) and (G)) shall be applied as conditions of approval for all
3              development approved under the expedited development review process.

4         3. Recreation Resources. The development shall not detract from the use and enjoyment of established
5         recreation sites on adjacent parcels.

6         4.   Natural Resources.

 7             a. Water Resources (Wetlands, Streams, Ponds, Lakes, and Riparian Areas). The development is outside
 8             water resources and their buffer zones. This guideline shall not apply to lot line adjustments or
 9             development located inside road, utility or railroad rights-of-way or easements that have been previously
10             disturbed and regularly maintained.

11             b.   Sensitive Wildlife and Rare Plants.

12                  (1)   The development meets one (1) of the following:

13                         (a) The development is at least one thousand (1,000) feet from known priority habitats or
14                         sensitive wildlife sites (excluding sensitive aquatic species, and deer and elk winter range) and
15                         known rare plants; or

16                         (b) The development does not disturb the ground or is inside road, utility or railroad rights-of-
17                         way or easements or other areas that have been previously disturbed and regularly maintained;
18                         or

19                         (c) For sensitive wildlife, the development is within one thousand (1,000) feet of known
20                         priority habitats or sensitive wildlife sites (excluding sensitive aquatic species, and deer and elk
21                         winter range), but an appropriate federal or state wildlife agency determines (1) the priority
22                         habitat or sensitive wildlife site is not active, or (2) the proposed development would not
23                         compromise the integrity of the priority habitat or sensitive wildlife site or occur during the time
24                         of the year when wildlife species are sensitive to disturbance;

25                         (d) For rare plants, the development is within one thousand (1,000) feet of known rare plants,
26                         but the Washington Natural Heritage Program or a person with recognized expertise in botany or
27                         plant ecology hired by the applicant has determined that the development would be a least two
28                         hundred (200) feet from the rare plants.

29                  (2) Development eligible for expedited review shall be exempt from the field survey requirements
30                  for sensitive wildlife (Section 40.240.860(C)) or rare plants (Section 40.240.870(C)).

32   C.   Treaty Rights Protection Guidelines.

33     Proposed development reviewed using the expedited review process shall comply with the following treaty rights
34   guidelines:

35        1.   Proposed development shall not affect or modify any treaty or other rights of any Indian tribe.

36        2. The expedited development review process shall cease and the proposed development shall be reviewed
37        using the full development review process if a tribal government submits substantive written comments during
38        the comment period that identify the treaty rights that exist in the project vicinity and explain how they would
39        be affected or modified by the proposed development.



                                                                  4
1         3. Except as provided in subsection (C)(2) of this section, the GMA and SMA treaty rights and consultation
2         goals, policies and guidelines in Part IV, Chapter 3, Tribal Treaty Rights and Consultation, shall not apply to
3         proposed developments reviewed under the expedited review process.

4    D.   Procedure for Expedited Review Process.

5         1.   Applications.

6              a. Prior to initiating any use or development, which requires review and approval by the responsible
7              official or examiner, an application shall be completed pursuant to this section.

8              b. The responsible official shall accept and review the application pursuant to this section for
9              consistency with the appropriate guidelines.

10             c. Standard application forms shall be available from the department, and shall be provided to the county
11             offices for which this chapter is effective and the Forest Service.

12             d. Applications for uses eligible for expedited review shall include the information required for review
13             uses listed in Section 40.240.050. They shall also include elevation drawings if the proposed development
14             would be visible from a key viewing area. The drawing shall show natural grade and finished grade.

15        2. Acceptance of Applications. Applications shall be accepted pursuant to Section 40.510.020(A) through (C)
16        or 40.510.030(A) through (C).

17        3.   Notice of Development Review.

18             a. Within seven (7) days of the acceptance of an application, the responsible official shall issue notice of
19             a proposed development review. The notice shall provide the following information:

20                  (1)   The name of the applicant;

21                  (2)   The general and specific location of the subject property;

22                  (3)   A brief description of the proposed action;

23                  (4)   The deadline for rendering an administrative or examiner’s decision; and

24                  (5)   The deadline for filing comments on the proposed action.

25             b. The notice shall state that the application and supporting documents are available for inspection at the
26             county during normal working hours.

27             c.   The notice shall state the applicant must comply with all applicable local, state, and federal laws.

28             d. The notice and a fully complete application packet shall be sent to the Gorge Commission, Forest
29             Service, and the four (4) Columbia River treaty tribes. The notice shall be sent to applicable county or city
30             planning office(s), libraries and other agencies and interested parties that request a notice or that the
31             responsible official determines should be notified.

32        4. Comment Period. Any interested person or party shall submit written comments within ten (10) days from
33        the date a notice is sent. Comments received by a tribal government at any time during the expedited review
34        process shall be considered, to ensure that the proposed development or use does not affect or modify the treaty
35        or other rights of that tribe.

36        5.   Written Administrative or Examiner’s Decision.



                                                                 5
1              a. In making an administrative or examiner’s decision on a proposed use or development the responsible
2              official or examiner shall:

3                 (1)   Consult with the applicant and such agencies as the responsible official deems appropriate;

4                 (2)   Consider information submitted by the applicant and all other relevant information available;

5                 (3)   Consider all comments submitted pursuant to Section 40.240.050; and

6                 (4)   Solicit and consider the comments of the Forest Service.

7              b. The responsible official shall approve a proposed use or development only if it is consistent with the
8              standards applicable to the property that is subject to the proposed use or development and the purposes of
9              the National Scenic Act.

10                (1) In approving a proposed development action, the responsible official or examiner may impose
11                conditions as necessary to ensure consistency with the guidelines of this chapter.

12                (2) Conditions attached to approval of a proposed development action shall be recorded in county
13                deeds and records to ensure notice of the conditions to successors in interest. The administrative or
14                examiner’s decision shall include this requirement.

15             c. The responsible official or examiner shall issue an administrative or examiner’s decision on a
16             proposed use or development including findings of fact and conclusions of law and any conditions to
17             ensure consistency with standards applicable to the property that is subject to the proposed use or
18             development and the purposes of the National Scenic Act within the timeframes outlined in Chapter
19             40.510.

20             d. The administrative or examiner’s decision shall be dispositive unless a notice of appeal is filed in
21             accordance with Section 40.510.020(H) or 40.510.030(I). An applicant who chooses to proceed with an
22             approved development during the appeal period shall assume all associated risks and liabilities.

23        6.   Notice of Administrative or Examiner’s Decision and Opportunity to Appeal.

24             a. The responsible official shall send a copy of an administrative or examiner’s decision issued under the
25             expedited review process to the four (4) tribal governments, the Gorge Commission, the Forest Service,
26             and landowners within two hundred (200) feet of the perimeter of the subject parcel.

27             b. Any person wishing to appeal an administrative or examiner’s decision issued under the expedited
28             review process shall do so pursuant to Section 40.240.050(I).

29        7. Expiration of Approvals. Approvals issued under the expedited review process shall expire in accordance
30        with the standards for expiration of approvals for review uses in Section 40.240.050(H).

31        8. Changes or Alterations to an Approved Action. Changes or alterations to an approval issued under the
32        expedited review process shall be made in accordance with the standards for changes or alterations to approved
33        actions for review uses in Section 40.240.050(J).

5    E.    Non-Residential Uses Following a Disaster.

 6   Non-residential uses, except special uses in historic buildings, bed and breakfast inns on a parcel in the SMA,
 7   overnight accommodations, and commercial events, shall not be considered discontinued solely because the use is
 8   inactive for the time period to repair the original structure or rebuild the replacement residence or structure where
 9   the land use occurred prior to the disaster. Special uses in historic buildings and bed and breakfast inns on parcels in
10   the SMA are discontinued after the historic building in which the use occurred is destroyed. An existing overnight
11   accommodations or commercial events use may resume only in a completed replacement structure and be used as
12   required in the existing permit and only while the existing permit remains valid.

13   F.    Campground Temporary Occupancy Following a Disaster.

14   An existing campground may allow a landowner whose primary residence was destroyed by disaster to occupy a
15   camping space in the campground until ninety (90) days after the landowner can occupy their replacement dwelling.
16   If the replacement dwelling has not commenced within the time allowed in Section 40.240.170(C)(7), the camping
17   space occupancy shall cease immediately.

18   DG. Changes to Existing Uses and Structures.

19         Except as otherwise provided, any change to an existing use or modification to the exterior of an existing
20         structure shall be subject to review and approval pursuant to this chapter.

21         1. Expansion of Existing Commercial and Multifamily Residential Uses. In the Special Management Area,
22         existing commercial and multifamily residential uses may expand as necessary for successful operation on the
23         dedicated site, pursuant to Sections 40.240.800 through 40.240.900 to minimize adverse effects on scenic,
24         cultural, natural and recreation resources. Expansion beyond the dedicated site is prohibited.

25         2. Conversion of Existing Industrial Uses. In the GMA, existing industrial uses may convert to less intensive
26         uses. A less intensive use is a commercial, recreation or residential use with fewer adverse effects upon scenic,
27         cultural, natural and recreation resources.

28         3. Existing Development or Production of Mineral Resources. In the SMAs, uses involving the exploration,
29         development or production of sand, gravel or rock in the SMAs may continue if both of the following
30         conditions exist:

31              (a) The sand, gravel, or crushed rock is used for construction or maintenance of roads used to manage or
32              harvest forest products in the Special Management Areas; and

33              (b) A determination by the Forest Service finds that the use does not adversely affect the scenic, cultural,
34              natural or recreation resources.

35   EH.    Discontinuance of Existing Uses and Structures.

36     Except as provided in Section 40.240.170(C), any use or structure that is discontinued for one (1) year or more
37   shall not be considered an existing use or structure. Proof of intent to abandon is not required to determine that an
38   existing use or use of an existing structure has been discontinued.

39         1. Multiple Uses. An existing use or structure with more than one legally established use may discontinue one
40         of the uses without discontinuing the others.

41         2. Change in Use. An existing use or structure shall become discontinued if the use or use of the structure
42         changes.
