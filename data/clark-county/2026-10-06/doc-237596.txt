        BACKGROUND
        The 2026 Preservation ADA Improvements project will construct or upgrade sidewalk curb ramps to
        meet current Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) regulations prior to pavement preservation work
        including sidewalk, and curb and pavement restoration at various locations in Clark County.

        This bundle, Group 2 designed by a consultant, will include 85 added or improved ramps at intersections
        throughout the County. Other groups will be completed on different schedules, but construction is
        expected to begin for this group in Fall of 2026.

        Council approval of the attached Noticed to Contractors is needed to authorize bid advertisement of this
        project. This project will advertise for bids once the contract documents are completed.

        Review by applicable departments/offices:
        The plans and specifications were assembled and reviewed by Public Works Design, Transportation, and
        Project Management teams and leadership.

        Prior council discussions/direction/approval:
        This package was brought before Council (link to video) December 4, 2024, to approve the contract with the
        design consultant (HHPR) for the design of these ramps, and subsequently approved for contracting with the
        design consultant.(link to approval)
