        BACKGROUND
        In February 2027, the Solid Waste and Recycling Division will participate in facilitation and professional skills
        training to support workforce development, effective communication, and improved team performance. To
        efficiently procure these training services, Clark County utilized the State of Washington Department of
        Enterprise Services (DES) Workplace Learning & Performance training contract.

        The attached Interagency Agreement is a piggyback off the existing DES state contract, authorized under
        Chapter 39.34 RCW, allowing Clark County to access pre-qualified facilitators and standardized pricing
        without conducting a separate competitive procurement. Under this agreement, DES will coordinate and
        manage the training delivery, including facilitator services, course materials, and administrative support, while
        the County will coordinate participation and any course-specific needs.

        In addition to this training, the Solid Waste and Recycling Division is planning a more extensive, multi-module
        professional development training series in 2027. This expanded program will further strengthen
        organizational communication, collaboration, and leadership capacity. Leveraging the existing DES contract
        provides continuity in facilitation resources, supports long-term workforce development goals, and reduces
        administrative burden by avoiding multiple procurement processes.

        The total cost of the 2027 training is not to exceed $11,778.42, inclusive of DES contract administration fees,
        and remains below the County’s small-contract threshold. This approach provides a cost-effective, timely, and
        compliant method for delivering specialized training aligned with County workforce development goals.
