        BACKGROUND

        Background:
        Washington House Bill 2060 established a surcharge on documents recorded by the County Auditor’s office
        that is to be used to fund housing programs from low and extremely low-income households. This Interlocal
        Cooperation Agreement is a multi‑jurisdictional arrangement between Clark County and participating cities to
        govern the collection and use of Washington HB 2060 Affordable Housing For All Surcharge funds. The
        surcharge provides revenue for housing programs serving extremely low‑ and very low‑income households.
        This Agreement supersedes earlier agreements governing these funds by removing outdated capital and
        bond‑related allocations and prioritizing funds for shelter operations, transitional housing, and permanent
        housing, guided through a public grant‑application process and recommendations from the Community
        Action Advisory Board (CAAB).

        Clark County will continue to collect and hold the funds, distribute them annually for approved programs, and
        prepare required annual reports. The Agreement continues to define eligibility (households at or below 50%
        of area median income), maintains administrative authority under CAAB, and limits applicability to the Clark
        County portion of Woodland. It also outlines indemnification, legal jurisdiction, withdrawal procedures with
        180‑day notice, equitable distribution of assets upon termination, and allows amendments by mutual
        consent. The Agreement is effective January 1, 2026 through January 31, 2031. RCW 39.34.030 (Interlocal
        Cooperation Act) requires approval by the Council for an Interlocal Agreement to become legally binding.
        Review by applicable departments/offices:
