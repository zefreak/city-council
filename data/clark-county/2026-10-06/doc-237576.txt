        BACKGROUND

        Background:
        Since 2017, Clark County has been a member of the state imagery consortium. The state imagery consortium
        is managed by the State of Washington’s centralized technology services agency, Washington Technology
        Services (aka, WaTech). Clark County's membership to the state imagery consortium allows the GIS
        department to obtain high-resolution aerial imagery every two years at a significantly discounted rate (it is
        currently estimated that the county's membership to the state imagery consortium has saved the general
        fund at least $500,000 since joining the consortium in 2017). The high-resolution aerial imagery is utilized by
        a number of county departments for their state-mandated and other critical business functions. The imagery
        is also made available for the public to view on the County's MapsOnline website.

        WaTech has recently requested that Clark County sign a new service level agreement in order to renew and
        maintain the county's membership with the state imagery consortium through June 30, 2028. Kevin
        McDowell from the County Prosecuting Attorney's Office has reviewed WaTech's service level agreement
        2026-001 and has approved it for the County Manager's signature.
