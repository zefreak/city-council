    1.0      EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
             MacDonald-Miller Facilities Solutions (MMFS) is pleased to present the following Energy Services Proposal
             (ESP) for Clark County Public Service Center. MacDonald-Miller understands that Clark County is
             proactively working to address critical facility infrastructure needs, comply with the Clean Buildings
             Performance Standard, reduce utility consumption, and reduce carbon emissions. MacDonald-Miller values
             the opportunity to collaborate with Clark County on their efforts.
             Clark County selected MacDonald-Miller to conduct a targeted Investment Grade Audit (IGA) of the Clark
             County Public Service Center. This IGA scope was negotiated and agreed upon through mutual approval
             of the IGA Proposal dated August 9th, 2024, which includes Cost Effectiveness Criteria (CEC) specific to
             the IGA and the Energy Efficiency Measures (EEM’s) proposed. The EEM’s proposed herein either meet
             the CEC or have been selected by Clark County based upon other objectives/goals/priorities.
             This Energy Services Proposal (ESP) summarizes the findings of the IGA report and provides Clark County
             with the methodology to implement the recommended Energy Efficiency Measures (EEM’s) through an
             Energy Savings Performance Contract (ESPC). This proposal complies with the Main Energy Service
             Agreement (MESA) and “Attachment A” General Conditions for Washington State Energy Savings
             Performance Contracting, in place between Washington State Department of Enterprise Services and
             MacDonald-Miller Facility Solutions.
             Project Overview
             Based on the findings of the IGA report dated June 25, 2025 and direction from Clark County and the DES
             Energy Program, MacDonald-Miller recommends implementing the following Energy Efficiency Measures:
                 •   EEM-1 Replace Jaces, copy station data and migrate to new server
             Detailed scopes of work for the recommended EEM(s) are provided in Attachment I.

             The Guaranteed Maximum Project Cost (GMAX) will not exceed the price of $1,354,810. The total project
             cost including design, construction, project management, documentation, owner training, warranty, M&V,
             applicable taxes and DES fee, is $1,526,989. A detailed Project Budget Summary is provided in Attachment
             III.
             Project Benefits
             Implementation of the Energy Efficiency Measures outlined in this ESP will result in the following owner
             benefits:
                 • Upgrades the legacy building Jaces to modern, open-protocol devices which will improve occupant
                      comfort and improve long-term maintainability.
                 • Migrate the outdated JCI controls to a new supervisor
                 • Reduces your facilities EUI by 16.8 points. The current EUI is 76.6 (2/2026) and the EUI target is
                      61.6.
                 • Reduce annual natural gas consumption by 20,934 therms per year.
                 • Reduce annual electrical consumption by 191,062 kWh per year.

                 • Potential for Clean Building Performance Standard (CBPS) Early Adopter Incentive of $326,554 (These

    3.0      ENERGY SAVINGS GUARANTEE POLICIES AND PROCEDURES
             The Energy Savings Guarantee Summary for the proposed EEMs are located in Attachment VI and the
             Measure and Verification plan is located in Attachment VII
             Energy and Savings Guarantee Policies and Procedures Overview
             MacDonald-Miller Facility Services (MMFS) will guarantee any portion of a project over which it has direct
             control. Where we do not have direct control (such as occupancy schedules), we are prepared to work with
             the Customer to devise a method of Measurement and Verification, which will provide a high degree of
             assurance that the calculated energy savings are realized.
             Performance Assurance will consist of specific reporting tasks that MMFS will perform to verify that the
             systems are performing as specified. The intent of the verification is to measure and verify indicators on
             which the savings are based. These indicators and activities conform to the IPMVP and are outlined in the
             proposed Measurement and Verification plan (reference Attachment VII).
             MMFS will guarantee energy consumption savings, or energy generation for renewable energy projects,
             not energy cost savings. Prevailing utility rates, adjustments based on weather conditions, and changes in
             use corrections are used to calculate avoided costs based on the established baseline. The guaranteed
             savings are based on the total project, not on a measure-by-measure basis.
             Remedies When Actual Savings Are Lower Than Estimates
             MMFS takes responsibility to remedy any shortfalls by correcting system performance or making a
             monetary payment. Any shortfall in savings will be identified by our measurement and verification program.
             The root cause of the shortfall will be identified, and corrective action taken. If efforts are not successful
             and an annual energy shortfall occurs, MMFS will pay the Customer the difference based on the agreed
             value of the energy.
             Length of the Savings Guarantees
             MMFS understands facility uses change, occupancy fluctuates, and preventive maintenance priorities may
             shift over the savings lifetime for the project. As such, adjustments to the baselines may be made. 1 year of
             guarantees and Measurement and Verification services will be provided as part of the proposal. If the
             Customer wishes to purchase additional years of measurement and verification, MMFS will continue to
             guarantee the performance of the measures as long as the Customer continues to maintain and operate
             the facility as recommended.
             Reporting
             MMFS conforms to the International Performance Measurement and Verification Protocol (IPMVP). The
             proposed Measurement & Verification (M&V) plan is published in Attachment VII. The M&V plan identifies
             the IPMVP option that will be used to validate savings. A post project M&V report will be created to confirm
             that all trends are set up and the installed equipment are operating as intended. One year after the
             commencement of energy savings is issued, the 1-year M&V report will be generated and reviewed with
             DES and the customer. The 1-year M&V report will verify that the measures are still operating as intended
             and the guaranteed savings have been realized.
             There shall be no cost savings split between the Customer and MacDonald-Miller, all cost savings to remain
             with the Customer.

             Agreement (MESA) and “Attachment A” General Conditions for Washington State Energy Savings

    3.0      ENERGY SAVINGS GUARANTEE POLICIES AND PROCEDURES
             The Energy Savings Guarantee Summary for the proposed EEMs are located in Attachment VI and the
             Measure and Verification plan is located in Attachment VII
             Energy and Savings Guarantee Policies and Procedures Overview
             MacDonald-Miller Facility Services (MMFS) will guarantee any portion of a project over which it has direct
             control. Where we do not have direct control (such as occupancy schedules), we are prepared to work with
             the Customer to devise a method of Measurement and Verification, which will provide a high degree of
             assurance that the calculated energy savings are realized.
             Performance Assurance will consist of specific reporting tasks that MMFS will perform to verify that the
             systems are performing as specified. The intent of the verification is to measure and verify indicators on
             which the savings are based. These indicators and activities conform to the IPMVP and are outlined in the
             proposed Measurement and Verification plan (reference Attachment VII).
             MMFS will guarantee energy consumption savings, or energy generation for renewable energy projects,
             not energy cost savings. Prevailing utility rates, adjustments based on weather conditions, and changes in
             use corrections are used to calculate avoided costs based on the established baseline. The guaranteed
             savings are based on the total project, not on a measure-by-measure basis.
             Remedies When Actual Savings Are Lower Than Estimates
             MMFS takes responsibility to remedy any shortfalls by correcting system performance or making a
             monetary payment. Any shortfall in savings will be identified by our measurement and verification program.
             The root cause of the shortfall will be identified, and corrective action taken. If efforts are not successful
             and an annual energy shortfall occurs, MMFS will pay the Customer the difference based on the agreed
             value of the energy.
             Length of the Savings Guarantees
             MMFS understands facility uses change, occupancy fluctuates, and preventive maintenance priorities may
             shift over the savings lifetime for the project. As such, adjustments to the baselines may be made. 1 year of
             guarantees and Measurement and Verification services will be provided as part of the proposal. If the
             Customer wishes to purchase additional years of measurement and verification, MMFS will continue to
             guarantee the performance of the measures as long as the Customer continues to maintain and operate
             the facility as recommended.
             Reporting
             MMFS conforms to the International Performance Measurement and Verification Protocol (IPMVP). The
             proposed Measurement & Verification (M&V) plan is published in Attachment VII. The M&V plan identifies
             the IPMVP option that will be used to validate savings. A post project M&V report will be created to confirm
             that all trends are set up and the installed equipment are operating as intended. One year after the
             commencement of energy savings is issued, the 1-year M&V report will be generated and reviewed with
             DES and the customer. The 1-year M&V report will verify that the measures are still operating as intended
             and the guaranteed savings have been realized.
             There shall be no cost savings split between the Customer and MacDonald-Miller, all cost savings to remain
             with the Customer.
