        DEPARTMENT: INTERNAL SERVICES AND ART COMMISSION

        DATE: September 15, 2026

        REQUESTED ACTION:
        Request approval to repurpose the existing $4,458 2026 dark County Arts Commission Festival & Event
        Expenses allocation to support Arts Talk Live, a CCAC community outreach program, and to use the funds for
        program expenses including artist/speaker honorariums, venue rental, reception expenses, promotional
        materials, sound/technical support, visual aids/equipment, and related event supplies.

        S Consent
        D Hearing
        D County Manager

        BACKGROUND

        Background:
        During the 2026 annual budget process, Council approved $4, 458 in Budget Package BCC-03-26AD for
        dark County Arts Commission Festival & Event Expenses. The approved uses included artist honorariums,
        chairs and tents, portable restroom rental, sound system rental, supplies, and stage/sound crew. The 2026
        allocation has not yet been spent. The CCAC is requesting approval to repurpose the funds for Arts Talk Live,
        a new series of free public events held at locations throughout dark County. The program will connect
        residents with established and emerging artists who will share their creative work, professional experiences,
        and practical insights on topics such as marketing, audience development, exhibition opportunities, and the
        economic impact of the arts. Compatible existing categories such as artist honorariums, sound/technical
        support, and crew would remain available, while other funds could support venue rental, reception expenses,
        promotional materials, visual aids/equipment, and related event supplies. No additional budget capacity is
        requested.
