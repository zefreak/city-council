 3          A RESOLUTION relating to the suspension of the annual site-specific comprehensive plan review
 4   and amendment cycle from October 1, 2026, through October 30, 2026.
 5
 6           WHEREAS, on December 20, 1994, Clark County adopted a 20-Year Comprehensive Growth
 7   Management Plan through Ordinances 1994-12-47 and 1994-12-53 to meet the goals and requirements of
 8   Chapter 36.70A RCW (also known as the Growth Management Act “GMA”); and
 9
10           WHEREAS, Clark County subsequently adopted updated 20-Year Comprehensive Growth
11   Management Plans through Ordinances 2004-09-02, 2007-09-13 and 2016-06-12 to meet the goals and
12   requirements of Chapter 36.70A RCW; and
13
14           WHEREAS, RCW 36.70A.130(5)(b) requires Clark County to take legislative action to review and, if
15   needed, update and revise its comprehensive land use plan and development regulations by December 31,
16   2025; and
17
18            WHEREAS, RCW 36.70A.130 (1)(e) requires that any amendment of or revision to a comprehensive
19   land use plan conform to Chapter 36.70A RCW, and that any amendment of or revision to development
20   regulations be consistent with and implement the comprehensive plan; and
21
22            WHEREAS, RCW 36.70A.130 (2)(a) requires Clark County to schedule consideration by the Council
23   of revisions to the comprehensive plan no more frequently than once every year; and
24
25            WHEREAS, outside of the comprehensive plan periodic update process that the county is
26   currently engaged in, the county’s practice was to amend the comprehensive plan each year following a
27   15-month process pursuant to Clark County Code (CCC) 40.560.040 , and concluding with plan revisions
28   that would take effect January 1 of the year following consideration of the proposals for amendment;
29   and
30            WHEREAS, on September 6, 2022, the council adopted Res. No. 2022-09-03 that suspended
31   consideration and approval of annual comprehensive plan amendments and accompanying rezone requests
32   filed pursuant to Clark County Code (CCC) 40.560.040 from October 1, 2023, through September 30, 2025,
33   to allow for consideration and adoption of the 2025-2045 comprehensive plan consistent with RCW
34   36.70A.130 (2)(a); and
35
36            WHEREAS, on September 23, 2025, the council adopted Res. No. 2025-09-01 that suspended
37   consideration and approval of annual comprehensive plan amendments and accompanying rezone requests
38   filed pursuant to Clark County Code (CCC) 40.560.040 from October 1, 2025, through September 30, 2026,
39   to allow for consideration and adoption of the 2025-2045 comprehensive plan consistent with RCW
40   36.70A.130 (2)(a); and
41
42           WHEREAS, since the adoption of Res. No. 2025-09-01, the timeline for adoption of the 2025
43   periodic comprehensive plan update is now extended by approximately six months; and
44
45           WHEREAS, because the 2025 periodic update is now expected to be approved in mid-October 2026,
46   an extension of the suspension of annual plan amendment cycle through October 30, 2026, will maintain
47   consistency with RCW 36.70A.130(2)(a); and
48

 1           WHEREAS, the Council considered the Suspension of Annual Reviews at council time on July 22,
 2   2026; and
 3
 4           WHEREAS, the Council considered the Suspension of Annual Reviews at a duly advertised public
 5   hearing on September 15, 2026; and
 6
 7            WHEREAS, the Council took public testimony from interested parties, considered all the written and
 8   oral arguments, testimony and considered all the comments presented to the Council; and
 9
10           WHEREAS, the Council finds and concludes that suspending the processes for annual site-specific
11   comprehensive plan amendments and accompanying rezone requests for the 2026 cycle is required by RCW
12   36.70A.130(2)(a), and will further the public health, safety and welfare; now therefore,
13
14           BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CLARK COUNTY COUNCIL, STATE OF WASHINGTON, as follows:
15
16   Section 1. Findings. The recitals above are hereby adopted as findings in support of this resolution.
17
18   Section 2. Suspend. The schedule set forth in Clark County Code for consideration and approval of annual
19   comprehensive plan amendments and accompanying rezone requests is suspended from October 1, 2026,
20   through October 30, 2026, after which date pre-applications may be submitted pursuant to CCC
21   40.560.040(A)(2) for the 2027 annual review cycle, with Council approval of a final adopting ordinance to
22   occur no sooner than one year after the adoption of the 2025-2045 comprehensive plan to maintain
23   consistency with RCW 36.70A.130 (2)(a).
24
25   Section 3. Instructions to Clerk.
26
