BACKGROUND

Background:
Staff requests that the Council extend the existing suspension of the annual review plan amendment cycle
for 30 additional days, to expire on October 30, 2026.

On September 6, 2022, the Council held a public hearing and approved Resolution No. 2022-09-03. The
purpose of that resolution was to suspend the annual comprehensive plan amendment cycle as set forth in
CCC 40.560.010 from October 1, 2023, through September 30, 2025. The suspension was to allow the
county to complete the comprehensive plan 2025 periodic update consistent with RCW 36.70A.130(2)(a),
“whereby updates, proposed amendments, or revisions of the comprehensive plan are considered by the
governing body of the county or city no more frequently than once every year.” At that time, the periodic
update was expected to be complete by June 30, 2025.

After the adoption of Res. No. 2022-09-03, the timeline for adoption of the 2025 periodic comprehensive
plan update was extended by approximately one year. That resulted in the adoption of Res. No. 2025-09-01
which extended the suspension of the annual plan amendment cycle through September 30, 2026. Because
the 2025 periodic update is now expected to be approved in mid-October 2026, an extension of the
suspension of the annual plan amendment cycle through October 30, 2026, will maintain consistency with
RCW 36.70A.130(2)(a). The annual review plan amendment applications would be accepted beginning
November 1, 2026, to be adopted no sooner than one year after adoption of the comprehensive plan
periodic update. This resolution allows the necessary extension of the annual review suspension to occur.
