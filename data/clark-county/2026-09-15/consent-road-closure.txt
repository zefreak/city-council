        BACKGROUND
        Advanced Excavating Specialists, LLC proposes to construct new sanitary facilities for the City of Vancouver’s
        West Vancouver Phase 1 Sanitary Sewer Improvements project on Northwest Division Avenue from the
        intersection with Northwest 45th Street to the terminus of the roadway, and on Northeast Creston Avenue
        from the intersection with Northeast 45th Street to the terminus of the roadway. Construction will require
        excavation along the entire roadway. This construction requires a work area that will not safely accommodate
        the proposed work and two-way traffic simultaneously.

        The contractor understands the impact this work will have on the community, adjacent property owners and
        access for emergency vehicles. To reduce impacts on the community, the contractor will provide access for
        property residents and emergency vehicles.

        The contractor anticipates starting construction as soon as the Council has approved the road closures
        and the community has been notified in compliance with RCW 47.48.020.

        Review by applicable departments/offices:
        This resolution has been reviewed by the Transportation Division, the County Engineer, and Legal.

        Prior council discussions/direction/approval:
        There have been no prior Council discussions related to this action.

        COUNCIL POLICY IMPLICATIONS
        There are no Council policy implications resulting from this action. This agreement supports the Clark County
        Council’s practice of investing in the County road system by providing safe and efficient transportation
        systems for all County residents.



        ADMINISTRATIVE POLICY IMPLICATIONS
        There are no administrative policy implications resulting from this action. Specific closure dates and alternate
        route information will be provided to the public in accordance with RCW 47.48.020.



        COMMUNITY OUTREACH
        The contractor will post variable message signs five days prior to the start of the closure. The City of
        Vancouver has notified impacted property owners. The contractor will issue a news release and post project
        and closure information on its website, including a map of the detour route, prior to the start of the road
        closure. The contractor will also post this information on their social media profiles; if the contractor does not
        have social media profiles, they will notify Public Works staff. Public Works will share the contractor’s social
        posts related to this proposed closure or, if the contractor does not have social media profiles, will post about
        the closure. The contractor will notify emergency response agencies and coordinate with other impacted
        parties as needed as required by the standard County traffic control permit.
