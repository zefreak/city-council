SUBJECT: Recommendation to Approve American Medical Response (AMR) 2027 Rates

FROM: EMSD2 Administrative Board


BACKGROUND
The EMSD2 Administrative Board is recommending that the EMSD2 Board approve AMR’s proposed 2027
patient rates. These rates, effective January 1, 2027, must be submitted to the Office of the Insurance
Commissioner by November 1, 2026.

The Ambulance Service Contract allows the contractor to increase rates by up to 10% of the current base and
mileage, using the August CPI-U (Seattle/Tacoma/Bellevue) and other factors such as labor, fuel, expenses,
reimbursement changes, and market conditions. AMR reported that the August CPI-U increased 4.4% over the
previous 12 months, while their costs increased 13.7%. The proposed rates effective January 1, 2027 are listed
below.

The rate increases will be felt most by 16.8% of the payors in the non-contracted insurance and private
pay/self-pay groups.




The Admin Board requested additional information from AMR and evaluated the cost detail by category. The
Admin Board feels the increases are reasonable given all organizations are all experiencing the same increases
in costs.

Throughout the next year, the Admin Board will be working with AMR to explore ways to reduce costs of the
contract and understand what AMR’s strategy is to help keep costs down as well.

BUDGET IMPLICATIONS
None

RECOMMENDATION
The Admin Board is recommending EMSD2 Board approve the proposed 2027 rates.

ACTION REQUESTED
Request Board approval of the 2027 AMR rates and direct staff to report them to OIC by November 1, 2026.
