                               Amendment to Interlocal Agreement
                                     Amendment No 2

        Clark County Emergency Medical Service District #2 (“EMSD2”), an emergency medical
services district authorized under RCW 36.32.480, which is composed of Clark County
(“County”), and the cities of Battle Ground, La Center, Ridgefield, and Woodland (Cities), and
Clark County Fire District #6 (“CCFD6”), a municipal corporation duly organized pursuant to the
laws of Washington, entered into an Interlocal Agreement (“Interlocal”) on January 22, 2025, for
EMSD2 to provide CCFD6 funding for emergency medical service related items.


                                           RECITALS


        WHEREAS EMSD2 and American Medical Response Northwest, Inc. (AMR) entered
into a contract for Ambulance Services (“Contract”), effective January 1, 2026;


        WHEREAS the Contract with AMR allowed for Contract Administration Costs;


        WHEREAS EMSD2, the County, and the Cities entered into an EMS Interlocal
Cooperation Agreement for the Provision of Ambulance Transport Services (“Cooperation
Agreement”) on January 1, 2026;


        WHEREAS the Cooperation Agreement provides that County staff support EMSD2’s
administrative and regulatory functions;


        WHEREAS CCFD6 has the expertise to provide specific contract administration services
for EMSD2 and to perform those services. CCFD6 will perform the services as outlined in
Exhibit A of the Interlocal;


        WHEREAS EMSD2 does not have available staff to provide such services for the benefit
of EMSD2; and


        WHEREAS the Interlocal allowed for amendments executed in writing as mutually
agreed by both parties.

    NOW, THEREFORE, EMSD2 AND CCFD6 HEREBY AMEND THE INTERLOCAL AS
FOLLOWS:

    1. Recitals: The recitals are incorporated herein by reference into the body of the Interlocal
        Agreement between the parties.


    2. Compensation: Effective January 1, 2027, EMSD2 shall pay CCFD6 for performing the
        services as outlined in Exhibit B, upon receipt of a written invoice. The parties mutually
        agree that in no event may the cumulative total amount billed exceed $33,000.00
        annually, without prior written approval by EMSD2.


    3. Effect of Amendment: Except as specifically amended herein, all remaining terms and
        conditions of the original agreement, remain in full force and effect.

                                            Exhibit B: Payment

Effective January 1, 2027, EMSD2 shall pay CCFD6 for performing services set forth in Exhibit A upon
receipt of a monthly written invoice. The parties mutually agree that in no event the billing shall exceed
$33,000 annually without prior written approval by EMSD2. EMSD2 shall pay the invoices within thirty
(30) days of receipt.

Invoices shall include the following:
     Date
     Billed to Clark County
     If there is an address, it needs to be a Clark County Department Address
     Unique Invoice Number
     Breakdown of service charges
     Total
     “Pay to” must match Supplier name on W-9 and what is in Workday
