                               Amendment to Medical Program
                                Direction Services Agreement
                                       Amendment No 1

       Clark County Emergency Medical Service District #2, an emergency medical services
district authorized under RCW 36.32.480, which is composed of the unincorporated areas of
Clark County and the cities of Battle Ground, La Center, Ridgefield, and Woodland, (“District”),
and Marlow Macht, MD., Clark County Medical Program Director, doing business as Clark EMS
Physicians PLLC, a Washington professional limited liability company (“Contractor”), entered
into an agreement for medical program direction effective January 1, 2026.


                                           RECITALS


       WHEREAS the Contractor will provide all the required services as outlined in the original
scope of work;


       WHEREAS annual compensation increases will be memorialized by written amendment
to Exhibit B; and


       WHEREAS the Contract allowed for amendments executed in writing as mutually agreed
by both parties.


       NOW, THEREFORE, THE DISTRICT AND CONTRACTOR HEREBY AMEND THE
AGREEMENT AS FOLLOWS:


   1. Effective Date. This amendment is effective as of January 1, 2027.


   2. Compensation. Section 4 of the Agreement remains as stated. Exhibit B has been
       updated, which is attached hereto and incorporated herein by this reference.


   3. Effect of Amendment: Except as specifically amended herein, all remaining terms and
       conditions of the original agreement, remain in full force and effect.

                                           EXHIBIT B

                                 Payment Terms and Schedule

Terms:

Payment to the Contractor for the work described in this Agreement shall be paid within fifteen
(15) days of receipt (considered received upon delivery) of Contractor’s monthly invoice.

During the term of this Agreement, Contractor’s compensation is subject to an annual increase
equal to the annual increase of the Consumer Price Index “CPI” for the Seattle-Tacoma-
Bremerton area for the immediate, previous four (4) quarters. The current CPI is 4.5%.

The oversight fee will increase from $71,707.00 to a not to exceed amount of $74,934.00. The
increase will be implemented during the first billing cycle of 2027. The increased amount shall
be equally divided and payable in twelve (12) monthly payments.

Annual compensation increases will be memorialized by written amendment to this Exhibit B.
