                          Amendment to Ambulance Services Contract
                                     Amendment No 1

       Clark County Emergency Medical Service District #2, an emergency medical services
district authorized under RCW 36.32.480, which is composed of the unincorporated areas of
Clark County and the cities of Battle Ground, La Center, Ridgefield, and Woodland, (“District”),
and American Medical Response Northwest, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Contractor”),
entered into a contract for ambulance services effective January 1, 2026.


       WHEREAS the Contractor will provide all the required services as outlined in the original
scope of work;


       WHEREAS the Contract allowed for fee adjustments and may increase rates and adjust
up to 10% of the current base and mileage, using the August Consumer Price Index for all
Urban Consumers (CPI-U Seattle/Tacoma/Bellevue).;


       WHEREAS the Contract allowed for Contract Administration Costs increases annually
based on the greater of three percent or the August Consumer Price Index for All Urban
Consumers (CPI-U Seattle/Tacoma/Bellevue). The August CPI-U was 4.4%.;


       WHEREAS the District will increase liquidated damages amounts annually based on the
greater of three percent or the August Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U
Seattle/Tacoma/Bellevue).;


       WHEREAS the Contract allowed for amendments executed in writing as mutually agreed
by both parties.

       NOW, THEREFORE, THE DISTRICT AND CONTRACTOR HEREBY AMEND THE
CONTRACT AS FOLLOWS:

   1. Effective Date. This amendment is effective as of January 1, 2027, at 00:00:00.


   2. Response Time Exceptions and Exception Requests. Article 4, Section 5.f. is hereby
       amended and replaced with the following:
                 Access: The District recognizes three four specific conditions that limit access to
                 the location of a call are beyond the Contractor’s control. Specific conditions are

                limited to a) Contractor access blocked by train without an alternate route with
                equal or superior time of travel and without railroad crossing; b) Contractor
                slowed by following first responder unit to scene of call; c) Construction if not
                previously known by the Contractor, or if known, the Contractor did not have
                reasonable means to mitigate its impact; d) PSAP provides an incorrect scene
                address to Contractor. To qualify, the Contractor must provide sufficient
                documentation showing one of the three conditions listed above occurred.


    3. Fee Adjustments. Article 9, Section 1.c. is hereby amended and replaced with the
        following:

                Contractor may increase rates and adjust up to 10% of the current base and
                mileage, using the August July Consumer Price Index for all Urban Consumers
                (CPI-U Seattle/Tacoma/Bellevue) in conjunction with other factors such as labor,
                fuel, other expenses, reimbursement changes and/or current market conditions.
                Contractor and EMSD2 will meet in September August of each year of the
                agreement to review Contractor’s methodology in establishing rate increases.
                Contractor shall file by October 1st of each year of the Contract and maintain
                with the Contract Administrator a current rate schedule of all patient fees and
                charges applicable to this Contract.


    4. Appendices & Exhibits. Appendices C.1, C.2, D, and E, and Appendix C in Exhibit A
        have been updated, which are attached hereto and incorporated herein by this
        reference.


    5. Effect of Amendment. Except as specifically amended herein, all remaining terms and
        conditions of the original agreement, remain in full force and effect.
