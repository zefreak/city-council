                    EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICE DISTRICT #2 BOARD
                             MINUTES OF JULY 13, 2026
                                    1:00 P.M.
The Board convened a hybrid meeting at the Public Service Center, 1300 Franklin Street, Vancouver, 6th floor,
conference room 680, Vancouver, Washington. Board members Myrna Frantz, Dean Thornberry, and Sue
Marshall, Chair, attended in person. Board members Michelle Belkot and Eric Overholser attended via WebEx.

CALL TO ORDER
Chair Sue Marshall called the meeting to order.

ROLL CALL
Overholser – Present
Belkot – Present
Thornberry – Present
Frantz – Present
Marshall – Present

ACTION ITEMS
1. Approval of the minutes from April 13, 2026.

   ACTION: Frantz moved to APPROVE the minutes for April 13, 2026. Seconded by Belkot.
           All aye/Motion carried.

2. Approval of warrants for Q1 2026

   ACTION: Thornberry moved to APPROVE the Warrants for Q1 2026. Seconded by Belkot.
           All aye/Motion carried.

3. Approval of 2026 Fall Supplemental budget items

   DeAnn Cordes, Contract Administrator, summarized the changes to the budget needing approval and
   explained the sources of revenue.

   ACTION: Belkot moved to APPROVE the Fall Supplemental Budget items. Seconded by Overholser.
           All aye/Motion carried.

4. Approval of Amendment #1 to EMS Interlocal Cooperation Agreement

   Cordes provided an overview of the amendment being requested.

   ACTION: Overholser moved to APPROVE Amendment #1 to the EMS Interlocal Cooperation Agreement.
           Seconded by Belkot. All aye/Motion carried.

5. Approval to Amend the Bylaws

   Cordes summarized the changes to the Bylaws proposed to fulfill previous direction. Overholser requested a
   wording change in section 6, clarifying which staff would be responsible for the preparation of the budget.
   Discussion was had by the board on this change, with a consensus of listing the Contract Administrator
   instead of staff.

   ACTION: Frantz moved to APPROVE the amendments to the bylaws as amended above. Seconded by
           Belkot. All aye/Motion carried.

      • Conclusion
