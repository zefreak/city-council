Public Health Protections
• Water quality monitoring
  • Meeting Safe Drinking Water standards
     • Acute exposure to hazards through drinking water,
       especially for vulnerable populations (children,
       pregnant people, compromised immune systems)
     • Dynamic systems and ecological processes
     • Technical assistance to residents to make informed
       choices
