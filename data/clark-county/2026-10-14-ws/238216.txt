 Major Maintenance
▪ Projects to increase the life of existing assets
▪ Funded with Real Estate Excise Tax (REET2)
▪ 6-year running average of $3.5m/year
▪ Project Examples
   Parking Restoration and Preservation
   Structure Preservation
   Playground and Surface Repair and Replacement
   Trail, Pathway, and Access Preservation
▪ Includes Emergent Projects budgeting
   $300,000-350,000/year

Major Maintenance Highlights
▪ Frenchman’s Bar Regional Park
   Parking pavement restoration, playground replacement, new parking fee machines
▪ Lewisville Regional Park
   Bridges replacement, pavement restoration, Central Shelter replacement
▪ Salmon Creek Greenway Trail
   Repair and reroute to address flood washout
▪ Moulton Falls Regional Park
   Parking lot preservation, restroom replacement
