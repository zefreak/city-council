   3. TIME FRAME
      Length of time needed for staff presentation: 1 hour

      Scheduled date:

   4. TIMING CONSIDERATIONS:
      Council requested an overview of the Legacy Lands program and Conservation
      Futures funding, including how property acquisitions are prioritized, how they are
      developed, and historical acquisitions. The Natural Areas Acquisition Plan guiding
      this work will be updated as part of the parks system plan update.

   5. DESIRED RESULTS:
      Public Works will provide a general overview of the Conservation Futures levy,
      Legacy Lands program and the Natural Areas Acquisition Plan to demonstrate how
      the program has been functioning. Public Works requests Council input to help
      inform the development of the natural areas acquisition strategy as the parks
      system plan update kicks off.

   6. IMPACTS (COMMUNITY/FINANCIAL/STAFF/OTHER):
      Public Works will spend the next several months engaging with community
      members, partners, stakeholders, and leaders to begin updating the parks system
      plan. This effort will inform the natural areas acquisition strategy.

   7. POLICY IMPLICATIONS:
      The natural areas acquisition strategy will guide the acquisition of conservation land
      and help inform regional trail priorities over the next 20 years.

   8. ISSUES TO BE CONSIDERED:
      Public Works will present the current natural areas acquisition plan and seek input
      from council regarding conservation priorities.

   9. RECOMMENDED ACTION:
      This will be an informational session with historical information on the Legacy
      Lands program and the current outreach and engagement approach that will guide
      development of the plan.
