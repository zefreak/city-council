Please distribute and make public the following proposed amendment to the
Comprehensive Plan:

1.1.24 Clark County and participating jurisdictions shall work cooperatively to
implement a countywide transfer of development rights (TDR) program starting in 2027.
This program shall work with the community to establish sending areas that preserve
farmlands, forests, historical areas, and open spaces. The program shall also work with
the community to establish receiving areas that encourage appropriate development
projects and affordable housing opportunities. Preference is given to a program where
the County serves as the TDR bank and assesses land and development values using
current market valuation to assign TDR credits based on a pre-determined price per
credit (e.g. $25,000 per TDR credit). Payments into the TDR bank may be allowed outside
of receiving areas as a mitigation tool. This program shall begin with a community
roundtable discussion hosted by the County to hear from various jurisdictions and
stakeholder groups their visions for a successful TDR program.
