MEMORANDUM
TO:           Clark County Council
FROM:         Oliver Orjiako, Director
DATE:         October 1, 2026
SUBJECT:      Response to Agency Comments

The county has received several agency Comments since the adoption process began with the Planning
Commission. Staff has provided two memos one specific to comments from the Department of Commerce
and one that includes comments from Washinton State Department of Transportation, Regional
Transportation Council and Public Works.
This memo is intended to identify the specific changes that are being proposed to address those
comments with page and line number to the Comprehensive Plan Element, Appendix or Title 40 code
section.
Washington State Department of Commerce
Land Capacity Analysis – An appendix N has been created that includes the Vacant Buildable Lands
Methodology (VBLM) and the model output for the proposed plan. The analysis includes an estimate of
how housing units at each income level can be accommodated.
https://clark.wa.gov/media/document/236841

affordability and the strategies in the Housing Options Study and Action Plan intended to remove those
barriers. https://clark.wa.gov/media/document/237426

and code audit. https://clark.wa.gov/media/document/236836
Housing Element Amendments related to RDI https://clark.wa.gov/media/document/237616
