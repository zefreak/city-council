APPENDIX O
Housing Barrier & Strategies Matrix
Clark County’s 2022 Housing Options Study and Action Plan (HOSAP) committed the County to
a range of housing actions that address identified barriers to housing production within the
County.

   •   The County adopted development regulations in 2024 that address a first phase (Phase
       1) of those actions, and is proposing adoption of a second phase (Phase 2) of those
       actions in tandem with the 2025 Comprehensive Plan adoption.
   •   Additional HOSAP strategies beyond Phase 1 and 2 include actions grouped around
       Housing Options, Affordable Housing, Programs and Partnerships, and Advocacy,
       intended for future phases of implementation (Future).
   •   Additional actions beyond the HOSAP strategies are also proposed with the 2025
       Comprehensive Plan implementation (CPI), as included in the following table.
   •   Where relevant, actions are noted as affecting the low-density residential zones (R1
       zones) or the medium- and high-density residential zones (R zones).
