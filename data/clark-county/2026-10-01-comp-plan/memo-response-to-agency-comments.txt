MEMORANDUM
TO:            Clark County Council
FROM:          Oliver Orjiako, Director
DATE:          September 16, 2026
SUBJECT:       Agency Responses to Periodic Update Comp Plan Materials

The county has received several agency Comments since the adoption process began with the Planning
Commission. Below are the list of agencies and the proposed amendments to address the agency
comments. These are intended to clarify language and correct omissions.
Regional Transportation Council (RTC) provided comments and recommendations for draft revisions on
August 19, 2026. In response the County proposes:
        •   Adding a new section in the Transportation Element that identifies Highways of Regional
            Significance. (pg. 29)
        •   Clarify that MMLOS standards for active transportation facilities apply to all state-owned
            facilities that are Highways of Regional Significance
        •   Clarify that the comprehensive plan includes a financial shortfall strategy discussing how
            additional funds will be raised.
        •   Provide complete ADA Transition Plan inventory to RTC to complete certification process
Washington State Department of Transportation (WSDOT) submitted comments on September 15, 2026.
In response the County proposes:
        •   Adding a reference to the 2024 WSDOT Highway System Plan (HSP) in the Transportation
            Element
        •   Add the following in the description of the HSP:
               o Under Revised Code of Washington (RCW) 47.06.050: The State Highway System Plan
                   serves as a vital component of the statewide multimodal transportation plan, outlining
                   long-term program needs, financing strategies and system improvements. The 2024
                   HSP prioritizes the following funding approaches for WSDOT facilities as follows:
                        1. First fund operations maintenance, capital preservation and critical
                           programs such as required fish passage projects and support programs.
                        2. Then for any remaining funds, $2 should be spent on safety and efficiency
                           strategies for every $1 spent on highway expansion projects.
Public Works Request on September 9, 2026
        •   Public Works submitted a memo requesting an amendment to the traffic impact fee formula in
            CCC 40.620.010 due to the removal of the Traffic Impact Fee Technical Document, to ensure
            the pass-by factor is applied accurately and consistently.
