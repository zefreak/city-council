BACKGROUND

Background:
CCC 2.79.040 Allows “Once every two (2) years the sheriff’s office may submit the fee schedule to the board of
county councilors for proposed adjustments in accord with the county budget process.” It appears this has not
been done since 2017. This updated fee schedule accounts for the rise in labor and other costs associated with
this operation.


Review by applicable departments/offices:
This fee adjustment is for the Clark County Sheriff’s Office Civil Department. This department serves civil
process throughout the county. This fee schedule has not been updated in Clark County Code since 2017.

Prior council discussions/direction/approval:
This was introduced to council earlier in the year in a work session that was held on June 17, 2026.

These fees are part of Clark County Code 2.79.020

 3   AN ORDINANCE amending Clark County Code 2.79.020 relating to fees collected by the Sheriff’s Office
 4   for official civil service work.

 5          WHEREAS, the State of Washington establishes fees to be charged by the Sheriff’s Office for official
 6   civil services in RCW 36.18.040; and
 7         WHEREAS, RCW 36.18.040 authorizes the County legislative body to set amounts of fees that shall be
 8   collected by the Sheriff to cover costs of administration and operation; and
 9         WHEREAS, the County last updated these fees in 2017, and the costs associated with official civil
10   services have increased since that time; and
11        WHEREAS, the Council finds it appropriate that costs incurred for the Sheriff’s official civil services
12   should be borne by those seeking action by the Sheriff and not by Clark County taxpayers; and
13        WHEREAS, the Council finds it appropriate to update the fee schedule to better reflect current
14   administrative and operational costs while maintaining public access to civil process services; and
15        WHEREAS, the Council is considering this matter at a duly advertised public hearing and finds that
16   adoption of this ordinance will further the public health, safety, and welfare; now, therefore,

17   BE IT ORDERED, RESOLVED AND DECREED BY THE CLARK COUNTY COUNCIL, STATE OF
18   WASHINGTON, AS FOLLOWS:
