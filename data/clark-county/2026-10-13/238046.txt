        BACKGROUND
        Clark County, in accordance with RCW 39.34, enters into interlocal agreements with local jurisdictions to
        facilitate the shared use of equipment, labor, roadway construction and maintenance services, materials, and
        facilities support.

        will be five (5) years, with the option to extend the agreement for up to an additional fifteen (15) years, in
