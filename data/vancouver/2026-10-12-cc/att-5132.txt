2 | HB 1491 Zoning Code Changes
 HB 1491 - Overall
 • Intended to promote transit- oriented
   development and affordable housing near
   transit through higher density
   requirements, parking elimination and 50%
   TIF reductions, MFTE incentives, and
   required affordability thresholds
 • Only affordable housing and TIF reduction
   requires new Title 20 zoning code changes
 • State pre-emptive model code in place


3 | HB 1491 Zoning Code Changes Changes
HB 1491 - Impacted Properties
• Station areas near transit are defined to include
  properties within ¼ mile walkshed of all 51
  Vancouver BRT stations
• Properties within station areas can be exempted
  if previously zoned for sufficient FAR, such as
  downtown
• Further exemptions of up to 25% of parcels near
  other stations allowed
• Detailed mapping of non-exempt properties to
  be referenced and linked in code

HB 1491 - Housing Affordability Thresholds
Residential development on impacted properties
within ¼ mile walkshed of station areas must
include, for 50 years:
• At least 10% of the housing units affordable at
   60% AMI, or
• At least 20% of units affordable at 80% AMI, or
• At least 10% of units affordable at 80% AMI, and
   at least 10% of units have two plus bedrooms.



6 | HB 1491 Zoning Code Changes
HB 1491 - Transportation Impact Fees
• Transportation impact fees may
  be reduced by 50% for
  developments claiming a
  multifamily tax exemption that
  meets the minimum
  affordability thresholds
