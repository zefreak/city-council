                ORDINANCE NO. M- [Ordinance Number]
       AN ORDINANCE relating to Comprehensive Plan and Zoning for the City of

Vancouver and the Vancouver Municipal Code (VMC) Title 20 in matter related to

statutory requirements to facilitate housing near transit; amending VMC 20.450.030 and

20.530.040; providing for severability; and establishing an effective date.

       WHEREAS, the legislature through HB 1491 has adopted requirements and

incentives intended to facilitate development of affordable housing near frequent

transit, codified under RCW. and

       WHEREAS, the environmental impacts of the proposed zoning text changes have

been reviewed and determined to be nonsignificant pursuant to the State Environmental

Policy Act (SEPA). A Determinations of Non-significance (DNS) was issued on August 27,

2026 for the proposed zoning text changes; and

       WHEREAS, the Vancouver Planning Commission reviewed proposed changes at

duly advertised public hearing on September 8, 2026, and at that hearing voted 4-0 to

recommend approval of the zoning text amendments described herein for adoption to

the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, City Council conducted a duly advertised public first reading

proposing this ordinance on October 5, 2026 and a duly advertised public hearing on

October 12, 2025; and




ORDINANCE - 1
      WHEREAS, City Council finds and concludes that the proposed changes are

consistent with the policies and provisions of the Comprehensive Plan that encourage

orderly development within the community and the Growth Management Act pursuant

to the requirements of Chapter 36.70A. RCW; and

      NOW, THEREFORE,

      BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY OF VANCOUVER:

      Section 1. The Planning Commission findings and conclusions as set forth in the
      staff report for the September 8, 2026 public hearing, and Commission
      discussions at that hearing, and the findings and conclusions set forth in the staff
      memo for the October 12, 2026 City Council regular public hearings, and Council
      discussions at that hearing, are hereby adopted as the City Council’s findings of
      fact and legislative conclusions; and

      Section 2. Zoning Code Text Changes:

       a. VMC 20.450.030 Impact Fees, subsection I.2, which was adopted by Ordinance

           M-4533 is amended as follows:

           2. Exemption or waiver from impact fees for low-income housing pursuant to RCW

           82.02.060(2). Pursuant to RCW 82.02.060(2), the review authority may grant a total

           or partial exemption or waiver from impact fees for housing developments

           containing up to three dwelling units and qualifying as low-income housing as

           defined in this chapter, to be owned and occupied by, or leased to, low-income

           persons. Requests for exemption and/or waiver for four or more dwelling units

           must be approved by the city council. No such impact fee exemption and/or waiver

           shall be granted for any low-income housing that has been granted a property tax

           exemption pursuant to Chapter 3.22 VMC, Multi-Family Tax Abatement, unless

           located within a bus station area defined in VMC 20.530.040. Any such exemption

           or waiver shall be subject to:


ORDINANCE - 2
           a. Provision being made for payment of the impact fee from public funds other than

                impact fee accounts; and

           b. Adequate documentation that the housing meets appropriate standards regarding

                household income, rent levels, sales price, location, and number of units;



         b.     VMC 20.530.040 Station Areas, which was adopted by Ordinance M-4533 is

          amended as follows:

                C. Housing Affordability. Within Station Areas, development must comply with RCW

                36.70A.840, if applicable. An HB 1491 Impacted and Exempt Parcels map of specific

                parcels within station areas subject to this requirement is available at the Community

                Development Department Permit Center, and the Department website at:

                https://www.cityofvancouver.us/business/planning-development-and-zoning/land-use-

                development/. Residential development on parcels identified as impacted on this map

                shall be subject to the following:

                1.   Minimum thresholds. Impacted properties must include one of the following, to be

                     enforced through a 50-year covenant entered into with the City of Vancouver:

                     a.   At least 10% of the units must be affordable for households earning 60% of Area

                          Median Income (AMI) for 50 years, or

                     b.   At least 20% of the units affordable at 80% AMI for 50 years, or

                     c.   At least 10% of the units affordable at 80% AMI for 50 years, provided at least 10%

                     of the units have two or more bedrooms

                2.   Transportation impact fees may be reduced by 50% for developments claiming a

                     multifamily tax exemption that meets the minimum affordability thresholds set in

                     section 1.
