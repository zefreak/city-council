Why existing homes?
Existing homes are an important opportunity to address both GHG
emissions and the conditions residents experience in their homes
                                                      2023 Communitywide GHG Emissions
• Buildings account for 37% of the City’s
  communitywide emissions and residential energy
  drives the largest share
• At the same time, Vancouver’s homes are
  increasingly exposed to extreme heat, rising
  temperatures, wildfire smoke, and high energy
  burdens
• These pressures can create unsafe, uncomfortable,
  and costly living conditions

5 | Healthy & Climate-Smart Homes
Community Engagement - Homeowners
Homeowners want practical support to understand and act on home
improvements
• What does my home need?
  Home energy assessments or other tools to identify needs and prioritize
  improvements
• How do I navigate the options?
  Help understanding existing programs, incentives, and available resources
• Who can I trust to do the work?
  Access to trusted contractors and support through the process
• How can I afford it?
  Direct financial assistance is preferred over financing improvements through
  ongoing payments

12 | Healthy & Climate-Smart Homes
Key takeaways

• Existing programs provide an important foundation; opportunity to build on and
  complement existing resources
• Residents request support identifying which improvements are appropriate for their
  homes and navigating the process
• A whole-home, coordinated approach could help sequence improvements, better
  leverage existing programs and resources, and address multiple goals
• Single-family homeowners and smaller rental properties present potential
  opportunities for targeted City support, with different approaches needed for each
• City’s role will depend on available funding and staff capacity
