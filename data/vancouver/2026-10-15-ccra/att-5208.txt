Fee Overview
Projects typically incur a range
of fees
• Impact Fees – Parks, Traffic & Schools
• System Development Charges (SDCs) –
  Water & Sewer
• Plan Review Fees – Development Review &
  Fire
• Permit Fees – Development Review & Fire
• Inspection Fees – Development Review &
  Fire
• Miscellaneous Fees – e.g., State fee


 2 | Development Fee Update
Impact Fees & SDCs Overview
•      One-time fees charged new development to pay
       for the cost of public infrastructure needed to
       serve new residents and businesses
•      Designed to have growth pay for itself rather
       than burdening existing taxpayers
•      Funds improvements that add new capacity to
       the existing network, not for existing deficiencies
•      Calculated based on the projected demand a
       new project creates and planned capital
       investments over the next 20 years.
•      Kept in separate accounts and restricted to
       designated capital improvements


    3 | Development Fee Update
Permit and Plan Review
Fees
•    Distinct from Impact Fees and SDCs
•    Fees paid to cover costs of processing
     and reviewing development applications,
     and inspecting construction activity
•    Calculated based on cost recovery model
     including staff, systems and transaction
     fees; time is tracked by staff and
     periodically reviewed
•    Costs include review by staff across
     multiple City departments, including
     CDD, Public Works and Fire

    7 | Development Fee Update
Why Consider Updates Now?
The City risks underfunding necessary infrastructure
• Impact Fees and SDC rates have not been adjusted in recent years despite
  increasing costs of required infrastructure projects and growing backlog.
• Comprehensive Plan update includes new growth forecasts and an updated
  Capital Facilities Plan to serve growth over the next 20 years.
• State law changes require calculation of impact fees based on proportionate
  impact.
• Commercial and industrial development does not share burden of new open
  space investments.

12 | Development Fee Update
Impacts on New Development
Any increase in fees will make new development more expensive

• Existing market conditions not conducive to absorbing significant fee increases due
  to rising construction costs and stagnating rent

• Fees typically make up a larger percentage of development costs in smaller projects
  (middle housing)

• Increases will negatively impact residential developments, commercial, and
  industrial projects

• Slower development may result in reduced revenue to City

13 | Development Fee Update
Implementation Scenarios
Fee increases can be implemented to mitigate impact on new development

• Full increases vs capped increase shared among all fee types
• Immediate increase vs phased increase over multiple years
• Pause new increases until market conditions improve
• Expand discounts and exemptions – as allowed by statute – to cover more types of
  development – affordable housing, child-care, transit-oriented development
• KEY – Find balance between potential impacts to development and being able to
  fund necessary infrastructure projects
