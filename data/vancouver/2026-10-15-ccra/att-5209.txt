A. There is hereby established a maximum daily parking rate of $25.00, a maximum monthly parking rate of
$200.00, and a maximum event rate of $25.00 per event stay. These rates may be reduced administratively by the
city manager, or his/her their designee, pursuant to the guidelines below:

B. Any citizen 62 years of age, or older, may receive a “senior citizen discount” in an amount to be determined by
the city manager or his/her their designee, but in no event should the discount exceed 20 percent;

D. The parking rates may be changed to an amount to reach desired occupancy rates at parking meters payment
collection devices;

The city manager, or his/her their designee, is authorized to promulgate rules and regulations regarding the
administrative reduction of the maximum parking rates. The city manager, or his/her their designee, shall report
to the city council from time to time as requested by the city council, but in any event, shall report at least once
every year regarding these parking rates.

14 | Parking Update to CCRA October 2026
Scofflaw Program
The lack of a scofflaw program allows a small group to abuse system

• Provides accountability for vehicles with multiple parking infractions.
• Equalizes authority to enforce in-state and out-of-state vehicles.
• Improves compliance with on-street parking regulations.
• Reduces the current number of delinquent or scofflaw vehicles.
• Reduces the number of boots and tows required after the first year by increasing
  payment compliance.
• Harmonizes off-street and on-street authority.




15 | Parking Update to CCRA October 2026
Scofflaw Program
Definitions

• Scofflaw refers to a vehicle which habitually and contemptuously breaks the law
  without concern for the consequences.

• 4 or more citations unpaid for 45 or more days.

• 3 additional notice and due process steps.

• Addition of immobilization devices (boots) and impoundment as enforcement tools.


16 | Parking Update to CCRA October 2026
Scofflaw Program
Current Authority
• RCW 46.16A.120
    o Prohibits vehicle registration in Washington only if there are any outstanding standing, stopping,
      and parking violations.
• RCW 46.55.113
    o Allows impound of a vehicle with registration expired by 45 days or more.
• VMC 19.13.080
    o “Subject to other monetary penalties provided by law including, but not limited to, referral to a
      collection agency.”
• No additional enforcement options are available to address delinquent fines
  associated with out-of-state or no-plate vehicles. These vehicles account for 47% of
  all unpaid fines.
