Item 5: Scoring Criteria and Application Questions Workshop
Andrew Westlund facilitated a workshop to review and prioritize the grant scoring criteria and ensure
application questions align with the information the committee needs when reviewing grant
applications. The Committee identified paid lodging nights, seasonality and economic impact as key
priorities. Members discussed maintaining lodging nights as a highly weighted criterion while
strengthening verification of applicants’ estimates. Committee members supported encouraging
applicants to consult with Visit Vancouver or a similar organization to help validate projected lodging
nights and economic impact. Seasonality would continue to favor events occurring during off-peak
periods, with clearer guidance provided to applicants about how timing affects scoring.
The committee discussed removing alignment with the Destination Master Plan as a heavily weighted
scoring criterion because previous responses were often broad and required significant interpretation
by reviewers. Equity and environmental stewardship would remain considerations, with clearer scoring
guidance and examples provided to applicants. Members also supported evaluating transportation and
parking separately from waste reduction and other environmental practices so applicants can more
clearly address each area. These considerations would be incorporated more consistently across grant
types where applicable.
Committee members also reviewed geography and marketing radius, which were generally supported
without significant changes. Members agreed that uniqueness has value but overlaps with other
scoring areas and should therefore receive less weight. The Committee also supported reducing the
confidence criterion and focusing it on the reviewer’s confidence in the feasibility and reliability of
information presented in the application rather than personal preference for a proposal. Applications
receiving very low scores in areas such as equity or confidence may warrant additional Committee
discussion.
Andrew Westlund will revise the application questions and scoring guidance based on the discussion,
including clearer explanations of what constitutes higher and lower scores. Scoring information may
be incorporated directly into the instructions accompanying each application question so applicants
understand how responses will be evaluated. Revised materials will be distributed to the Committee
for review before being incorporated into the grant management system.

Item 6: Applicant Presentations and Recommendation Scheduling
The Committee reviewed the fall meeting schedule and agreed to cancel the August and September
meetings in anticipation of the additional time commitment required during the grant review process.
The October 14 meeting will be extended to approximately two and a half to three hours to review
compiled application scores and determine which applicants will advance to the presentation round.
Staff noted that applicants selected to advance will need sufficient notice to schedule their
presentation time and prepare materials.
The Committee discussed scheduling three potential presentation dates, with the expectation that only
two may ultimately be needed depending on the number of applicants advancing. Based on member
availability, staff proposed placing holds on October 21, October 29 and November 6, with October 29
potentially canceled if only two presentation sessions are necessary. The Committee will also need a
subsequent meeting to finalize funding recommendations for City Council. Members discussed
November 12 and November 18 as potential dates, as well as the possibility of combining the final
presentations and recommendation discussion into an extended November 6 meeting.
