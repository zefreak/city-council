Legislative Requirements
HB 1491- Transit Oriented Development
• Mandatory 20-year MFTE option within ¼ mile of station areas
• 50-year affordability compliance term
• Affordability requirements :
     − 10% of units @ 60% AMI, or
     − 20% of units @ 80% AMI, or
     − 10% of units @ 80% AMI if at least 10% of units are 2 bedroom or larger
• MFTE certificate approvals are issued administratively
• Recorded covenant deed restriction required
• 50% reduction of traffic impact fees
