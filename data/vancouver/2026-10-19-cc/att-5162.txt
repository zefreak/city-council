      AN ORDINANCE amending the Vancouver Cultural Access Program

      WHEREAS, on December 2, 2024, the Vancouver City Council (“Council”)

approved ordinance M-4481 to establish the Vancouver Cultural Access Program,

approve a one-tenth of one-percent sales tax for this purpose, and designate the

Culture, Arts & Heritage Commission (“CAHC”) as the “designated entity” to approve use

of the funds; and

      WHEREAS, a community task force guided by the CAHC created a Vancouver

Cultural Access policy, which established a grant program among other things. The

policy was reviewed and recommended by the CAHC and approved by Council on

September 15, 2025. On July 20, 2026, Council approved an amendment to the grant

program; and

      WHEREAS, three currently serving CAHC commissioners have applied for grants

under the grant program; and

      WHEREAS, to ensure integrity of the grant program, an amendment to

Chapter 3.45 of the Vancouver Municipal Code is necessary to ensure that currently

serving CAHC Commissioners do not receive a direct or indirect benefit from the

issuance of any grant funds.

      NOW, THEREFORE,

      BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY OF VANCOUVER:


ORDINANCE - 1
      Section 1. Section 3.45.040 Designated Entity is amended as follows:

      A. Pursuant to RCW 36.160.060, the city designates the culture, arts, and heritage
      commission (CAHC) established under Chapter 2.88 VMC as the designated entity
      under RCW 36.160.020(4).

      B. A non-profit organization is prohibited from receiving funds issued by the
      CAHC in its role as the designated entity if a currently serving CAHC
      commissioner will receive a direct or indirect benefit from the issuance of those
      funds. “Direct benefit” includes funds issued to a non-profit organization for
      which the commissioner is a board member, employee, or paid contractor
      providing services to the non-profit organization. “Indirect benefit” includes funds
      issued to a non-profit organization through a fiscal sponsor in which a CAHC
      commissioner serves as a board member or employee of the fiscal sponsor.

      C. The CAHC shall collaborate with the school districts in the city to develop and
      provide a public school cultural access program (PSCAP) as provided in RCW
      36.160.100 and 36.160.110(4).

      Section 2. Severability. If any part of this Ordinance or its application to any

person or circumstances as determined by a court with jurisdiction over the matter to be

invalid, that order or judgment will be confined to the controversy in which it was

rendered and will not affect or invalidate the remainder of the Ordinance to any other

person or circumstance, and to this end, the provisions of each clause, sentence,

paragraph, section or part of this Ordinance are severable.

      Section 3. Effective date. This ordinance is effective on October 19, 2026.

                              ORDINANCE NO. __________

       AN ORDINANCE amending Section 3.45.040 Designated Entity of the Vancouver

Municipal Code to prohibit the receipt of grant funds by a non-profit organization under

the Cultural Access Program if an appointed Culture Arts and Heritage Commission

commissioner would receive a direct or indirect benefit from that issuance.
