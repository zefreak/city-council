     RE:            Recommendation for Appointment to the Culture, Arts, and Heritage
                    Commission

     The City’s Culture, Arts, and Heritage Commission is a nine‐member volunteer group
     responsible for facilitating the development and promotion of a thriving cultural, art
     and heritage environment that reflects the people we serve. This is accomplished
     through the creation of community‐informed programs, ownership of buildings and
     public art, and a commitment to community partnerships.

     Council Subcommittee 1 recommends the mid-term appointments of Shannon Stewart
     and Carmen Garcia Marshall with terms effective immediately and expiring December
     31, 2027.

     This appointment will be presented for Council action at the Monday, October 19, 2026,
     Council meeting.
