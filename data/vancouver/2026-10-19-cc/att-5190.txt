Community Engagement
   •   HART connected with dozens of community members at the annual Open House Ministries Block Party,
       providing information about HART services. The event was featured in the Columbian newspaper on




        P.O. Box 1995 | Vancouver, WA 98668-1995 | 360-487-8000 | TTY: 711 | cityofvancouver.us
      August 13th. ‘It really is about relationships’: Open House Ministries’ annual block party reaches out to those in
      need - The Columbian
  •   Vancouver Defenders hosted an experiential learning session where HART met with 10 defense
      attorneys and staff to discuss the intersection of criminal justice, housing, homelessness, community
      court, and other challenges experienced by our mutual clients.
  •   Butler County, Ohio Commissioner Cyndi Carpenter visited the City of Vancouver and met with
      members of the City’s Homeless Response program to learn how Vancouver is supporting our
      unsheltered community and working to improve local conditions.
  •   2026 Veterans Stand Down & Expo: HART connected with hundreds of Veterans and their family
      members through our outreach table. Outreach Coordinator Lon Klugman was also featured in The
      Columbian’s coverage of the event. https://www.columbian.com/news/2026/sep/25/annual-stand-down-
      event-provides-veterans-clothing-services-camaraderie/
  •   Annual Youth Forum & Future Civic Leaders Event: Hosted at Rooted School with students in grades 9–
      12, the event brought together the City of Vancouver, City Council members, and students for civic
      engagement. HART joined grades 9–11 for an interactive discussion about homelessness, inviting
      students to share their ideas for strengthening our community.

Appreciation
We are proud to recognize the Information Technology Department for their exceptional partnership and
ongoing support of the City's response to homelessness. The work of responding to homelessness increasingly
depends on technology, communication, mobility, and the ability to adapt quickly in the field. IT consistently
meets those demands by providing outstanding technical support, managing and updating equipment, and
developing solutions specifically tailored to the needs of our team. Their contribution goes beyond
technology. IT demonstrates the City's commitment to innovation by approaching challenges not simply as
problems to solve, but as opportunities to improve. They repair bridges when necessary, but more
importantly, they build bridges— between departments, between people, and between the challenges we
face and the solutions we need. Their professionalism, creativity, and "can-do" spirit are an example of what
effective partnership looks like. We thank the Information Technology Department for the important work
they do every day and for helping empower our team to serve our community and advance the City's mission.
In recognition that the City’s Homelessness Response team could not accomplish what it does alone, especially
during this emergency, HART would like to express its appreciation for the many departments, divisions, and
individuals who partner in the work:
