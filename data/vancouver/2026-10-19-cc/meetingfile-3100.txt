Subject
Interlocal Agreements with Department of Social and Health Services and Developmental Disabilities
Administration for Respite in Community Settings and Recreation Opportunities

Key Points

     • Vancouver has partnered with the State of Washington in the Respite in Community Settings
       and Recreational Opportunities programs via these interlocal agreements for over 20+ years.
     • The programs allow qualified individuals living with a disability to participate in existing
       Vancouver Recreation programs while the State pays the fees directly to the City through
       these agreements.
     • Participation in this program brings additional revenue and participation to PRCS programs
       and services.


Strategic Plan Alignment
Vibrant and Distinct Neighborhoods – a variety of accessible places and spaces

Safe and Prepared Community – a safe place to live, work, learn and play

High Performing Government – a government that is reliable, fiscally responsible, equitable, and
open to compromise

Present Situation
The State of Washington Department of Social and Health Services (DSHS) and Developmental
Disabilities Administration (DDA) administer the Respite in Community Settings & Recreation
Opportunities Interlocal agreements. This program allows eligible participants living with a disability to
utilize funds from these programs to participate in existing specialized and general recreation and
enrichment programs and activities offered by Vancouver Parks, Recreation & Cultural Services
(PRCS). This opportunity also benefits families and caregivers by providing respite time for them
while knowing their person is in a safe and supportive environment.

 VPRCS has partnered with DSHS & DDA in these interlocal agreements to serve the neurodiverse
and/or individuals living with a disability in the community for numerous agreement cycles. As a
result, VPRCS has been and continues to be the preeminent provider of recreation and enrichment
programming for our neurodiverse community members and those in the community living with a
cognitive disability in Southwest Washington. This program enhances overall PRCS programs and
services by increasing participation and revenue which allows us to offer a broader range of
programs.

Advantage(s)


                                                                                                Page 13 of 131
    1. Provides eligible community members living with a disability access to use funds for
       meaningful, safe and accessible recreation, enrichment and wellness programs and activities.
    2. Provides families who are supporting an individual(s) with a disability with the ability to have
       important regular respite time and opportunities.
    3. Provides revenue for Vancouver recreation programs, including the Access to Recreation
       (ATR) program, which serves individuals with cognitive disabilities.


Challenge(s)
None

Budget Impact
No budget impact. The agreement covers full cost for eligible individuals to participate in existing
PRCS recreation programs and services.

The last Council action was November 13, 2023.

community partnerships.
