                             LIMITED DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY FROM CITY MANAGER TO
                            ANDY MEADE, RECREATION PROGRAM AND FACILITY MANAGER

             In accordance with Vancouver City Charter Section 3.03 (City Manager – Powers and Duties) and
             the authority delegated to me by the Vancouver City Council, I, Lon Pluckhahn, City Manager,
             hereby delegate to Andy Meade, Recreation Program and Facility Manager for the City of
             Vancouver Parks, Recreation, and Cultural Services Department, the authority to execute on
             behalf of the City of Vancouver certain interlocal agreement client services contracts with
             State of Washington Department of Social and Health Services for the purpose of providing
             Respite in Community Settings & IFS Recreational Opportunities services to clients of the
             Developmental Disabilities Administration and to execute all supporting documents associated
             with such client services contracts, as required by the State of Washington.
             This limited delegation of authority is specific to the designated individual, Andy Meade, and is
             effective until January 1, 2029 unless otherwise earlier revoked or terminated.
