   Getting Started
            Carefully review the requirements for the posted position before completing your application.
            Review board information including membership eligibility and expectations to confirm qualifications.
            Commission meetings may require in-person day or evening attendance. When you accept a
            Board/Commission seat, you’ll confirm your ability to attend these meetings.
            Upon appointment, proof of identity and residency may be required (“Do I live in Vancouver City limits?” map)
            Applications are considered active for one year. You will be notified by email of future openings for those Boards
            and Commissions you previously applied for or expressed interest in.
            Under the state’s Public Records Act (RCW 42.56), applications submitted for these appointments are public
            records and may be subject to public disclosure.
            City employees are not eligible to serve on boards and commissions.
