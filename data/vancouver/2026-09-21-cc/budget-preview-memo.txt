Re:           Preview of the City Manager’s Recommended 2027-2028 Biennium Budget



The purpose of today’s workshop is to preview the City Manager’s Recommended
Biennium Budget for 2027-2028. Although not all decisions are finalized, the Biennium
Budget is in a position where a high-level preview can be discussed with City Council.
This is a new practice and reflects more intentional conversations with the City Council
about the budget.

With the previous presentation dates of April 27, July 13, and August 24, today’s
workshop represents the fourth workshop on the budget with the Council.

During today’s workshop, we will discuss the high-level work that will be continued in
the budget, such as the near-term goals of the Comprehensive Plan implementation, the
Heights project, the Arts Hub capital project, supporting the Bridge Shelter, and others.
We will also share some of the future work that will be planned, such as annexation
preparation, regional fire authority exploration, electric vehicle infrastructure
construction and Boards and Commission support.

The workshop also includes intentional reflection points to further discuss Council’s
thoughts on HB 2442, a new local sales tax of .01% to fund local children and family
services. Currently, it is programmed within the budget to be implemented in 2028, with
some of the sales tax to offset expenses for current Children Justice Center costs and
other resource support with capacity for other programming subject to Council
direction.

Summary

This workshop is intended to be a preview of the City Manager’s Recommended
Biennium Budget for 2027-2028 and thus does not represent the final budget. This
workshop is an opportunity to share and discuss the budget's direction with the City
Council and receive feedback as it is finalized. After this workshop, the budget will be
finalized as a Recommended Budget for publishing on October 1.
