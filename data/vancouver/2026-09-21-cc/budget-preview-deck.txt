8 | City Manager Recommended Biennium Budget Preview
Overview
27-28 City Manager Recommended Budget

• Assuming all things equal, forecast very conservatively
• Holding department spending patterns
• Setting up for the next four years
• Implemented measures: using existing funding for vehicles, full study of
  vehicle acquisition, organizational resiliency, Prop 2 banked capacity,
  leveraging of existing funding
• Prepares for the future: debt service, capital projects, annexation
• Continues the work: Comprehensive Plan implementation, Homelessness
  Support/Outreach, Public Defense, Affordable Housing
