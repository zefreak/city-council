                     COVINGTON HOUSE USE AGREEMENT

        THIS COVINGTON HOUSE USE AGREEMENT (this “Use Agreement”) is entered
into by and between the City of Vancouver, a municipal corporation of the State of Washington
(the “City”) and the Clark County Historical Society, a Washington non-profit corporation (the
“Society”) as of October 1, 2026 (the “Effective Date”) for the benefit of the public.

       WHEREAS, the City has owned the structure, commonly known as the Covington
House, located on an area depicted as the “Covington House Site Limits” (the “Property”) on
Exhibit A since 1926; and

      WHEREAS, the Covington House, built in 1846, is the oldest extant house in the State of
Washington and was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1972, and

       WHEREAS, the Vancouver School District (“VSD”) owns the land upon which the
Covington House is located and VSD and City have a Use Agreement that allows the City to
continue to occupy and engage in limited use of the Covington House and associated grounds
through November 30, 2027, attached to this agreement as Exhibit B (the “VSD Use
Agreement”); and

       WHEREAS, the Covington House Heritage Society, a Washington non-profit
organization formerly known as the Vancouver Women's Club, has licensed use of the Property
from the City since 1927; and

       WHEREAS, the Clark County Historical Society, a Washington non-profit corporation,
whose mission is to “Gather, Save and Share the stories of the Clark County community,” has
successfully operated the Clark County Historical Museum, at 1511 Main Street, since 1963; and

        WHEREAS the Society represents and warrants to the City that the Covington House
Heritage Society has determined it will terminate its non-profit status and transfer its remaining
assets to the Clark County Historical Society, and:

        WHEREAS, the Clark County Historical Society and the City wish to enter into this Use
Agreement providing terms and conditions for the continued public benefit by providing public
access in support of economic and social stability and for historical preservation of the
Covington House.

       NOW THEREFORE, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. Premises.
The City hereby authorizes the Society to use a portion of the Property located at 4201 Main
Street known as the Covington House, which includes the building, storage shed, surrounding
yard and parking lot (the “Premises”) subject to and in a manner consistent with that authorized
and allowed by the VSD Use Agreement.

Section 2. No Cost Use and Public Benefit.
The Society shall pay $1.00 per year to the City for this Use Agreement because the Society, is a
501(c)(3) non-profit organization committed to responsible stewardship, innovative collaboration
and inspiring exhibitions and programs that engage the community through operations at the
Clark County Historical Museum and, under pursuant to this Use Agreement, by assisting in the
economic and social stability of the community through preservation and interpretation of the
City's cultural heritage and by making the Covington House available for public use, including
for low-income persons, through Clark County Historical Museum extension programming at the
Premises.

Section 3. Condition of Premises.
The Society, through this Use Agreement, accepts the Property in its present condition and
assumes full financial responsibility for costs associated with its operations including staffing,
operating supplies and equipment. The City agrees to continue to maintain the Covington House
and associated grounds including the parking lot and yard surrounding the house in a manner
consistent with its condition as of the Effective Date.

Section 4. Admission Fees and Building Operations.
The Society shall have the authority to charge reasonable rental fees for the use of the Covington
House based upon recovery of the Society's janitorial services and operational costs incurred
exclusively for the Covington House. The Society shall have authority to establish policies and
make rules and regulations pertaining to the public use of the building and grounds to ensure
proper use, care and protection consistent with the Society’s operation of the Clark County
Historical Museum.

The Society agrees to maintain a website for the Covington House, including current contact
information, and, consistent with the public benefit purpose of supporting economic and social
stability for low-income persons in the community, agrees to promote and hold up to five (5) free
public open houses at the Covington House per year.

The Society also agrees that it will allow any person or organization to use the Premises when
such use is not in conflict with use by the Society and subject to any rules or regulations for such
use as established by the Society and/or City. All such uses must be scheduled through the
Society.

The Society shall be responsible for any damage or loss resulting from the Society’s operation
and use of the Premises.

Section 5. Janitorial Services and Utilities.
The City will provide ordinary and customary basic janitorial services on a routine basis, and
garbage, sewer, water, and electricity utility services. The Society will provide additional
janitorial services that may be required for special event rentals authorized by the Society.

Section 6. Alterations and Capital Improvements.
Alterations or improvements to the interior of the Premises may be made by the Society upon
written approval by and under the supervisions of the Facilities Division of the City's General
Services Department, which approval may be granted or withheld at the City’s sole discretion.

Any such improvements will be subject to then current Washington State Prevailing Wage
Requirements for Public Works pursuant to Chapter 39.12 RCW. Any permanent improvements
made during the term of this Agreement shall become a part of the Premises and shall not be
subject to removal if such removal would in way damage or deface the Premises.

No improvements to the grounds or landscaped areas shall be made, as these areas must remain
accessible to the City's Grounds Division for maintenance and operational purposes.

The City will be responsible for capital improvements resulting from normal wear and tear
including replacement of roof, floor, mechanical, electrical and plumbing. The City may provide
for structural and other major improvements to the building and grounds as funds are available.

Section 7. Annual Report.
The Society will provide to the City an annual report, due each year thirty (30) days after the
Effective Date, that includes the following information:

   a) Current board of directors list;
   b) Current annual operating budget for the Society;
   c) A copy of the most recent Form 990 (short form/postcard acceptable)
   d) Number of public events, including open houses, sponsored by the Society or other
   e) organizations held at the Covington House in the previous year;
   f) Number of event rentals at the Covington House in the previous year;
   g) An estimate of the number of visitors to the House.

Section 8. Hold Harmless.
The Society will indemnify, defend and hold harmless the City and any agents or employees of
the above, from any claim, liability, damage or loss occurring on or about the Property and
arising out of any activity by any volunteer, guest or employee of Society while on the Property
or in the building or resulting from Society's failure to comply with any terms of this Agreement.
The City will have no liability to Society because of loss or damage caused by acts or omissions
by third parties. Society's indemnity shall extend to all costs of defense, including without
limitation the attorneys' fees of the City, or its respective agents or employees.

In the event the obligation to indemnify is caused by or results from the concurrent negligence of
the City or its agents or employees, and the Society or its employees or agents, the Society shall
be required to indemnify the City only to the extent of negligence of the Society or its employee
or agents.

Section 9. Insurance.
The Society will obtain and keep in force a policy of comprehensive general liability insurance
covering the agents, employees, volunteers and guests of the Society against damage or liability
arising out of the use or occupancy of the Premises and areas appurtenant to the Premises. The
policy will be in the amount of not less than $2,000,000, combined single coverage, including
property damage. Coverage limits of the insurance policy will not limit the liability of Society
under this Agreement. Society will be responsible for any deductibles under the policy.

Within five business days of the Effective Date, the Society will furnish the City a certificate
evidencing the required insurance described in Section 7(a). The certificate will state that
coverage will not be canceled or materially changed without thirty (30) days' advance written
notice to the City.

The Society must name the City as an additional insured on the insurance policy required by this
Use Agreement. The Society will provide a renewal certificate at least thirty (30) days prior to
expiration of any policy. Failure to maintain the required coverage and provide the required
documentation as outlined in this section is deemed a material breach of this agreement and will
result in the termination of this agreement if not corrected within ten (10) days from the day the
City provides notice of the breach.

Section 10. Assignment.
The Society shall not assign or sublicense the use of any part of the Premises without the written
consent of the City, which consent shall be granted or withheld at the City’s sole discretion.

Section 11. Term.
To coincide with the VSD Use Agreement, the term of this Agreement shall be from the
Effective Date through November 30, 2027. If the City and VSD extend the VSD Use
Agreement beyond November 30, 2027, the City and Society may concurrently extend this Use
Agreement by mutual consent, provided the Society is not in default of this Use Agreement.

Section 12. Termination.
Either party may cancel this Agreement by providing 180 days advance written notice of such
intent.

Section 13. Notices.
Notices between the Parties related to this Agreement shall be in writing to the address for the
party stated below or such other address as either party may specify by notice to the other.

access to the Premises on the basis of age, sex, marital status, sexual orientation, gender identity,
race, creed, color, national origin, citizenship or immigration status, honorably discharged

                  USE OF KIGGINS BOWL PROPERTY BY COVINGTON HOUSE

        This is an agreement by and between the CITY OF VANCOUVER, a municipal
corporation of the State of Washington, hereinafter referred to as "City", and VANCOUVER
SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 37, hereinafter referred to as "District", by which the District allows
the City to use a portion of the property known as Kiggins Bowl which is now and will continue
to be occupied by Covington House.

        WHEREAS, on June 28, 1993 the City and the District entered into a Property Exchange
Agreement whereby the City agreed to convey to the District property commonly known as
"Kiggins Bowl", including that portion of the property which is occupied by Covington House, and
the District agreed to convey to the City certain property at Central Park.

      WHEREAS, on November 15, 1993 and December 17, 1993, the City and the District
exchanged deeds to the properties mentioned above;

        WHEREAS, the Vancouver School Board, on 11/08/93, authorized the District to enter
into this Agreement and the Vancouver City Council, on 11/15/93 authorized the City to enter
into this Agreement; and

        WHEREAS, the City owns the structure known as the Covington House which is located
on an area depicted as the "Covington House Site Limits" property ("Property'') on the attached
Exhibit A; and

       WHEREAS, the District agreed to allow the City to continue to occupy and use the
Property, and the City agreed to maintain the Property and Covington House; and

       WHEREAS, the City and District entered into a Use Agreement commencing on November
11, 1993 and expiring November 10, 2003 ['Expired Agreement''); and

      WHEREAS, the City and District entered into a Use Agreement commencing on
September 18, 2009 and expiring on December 31, 2013 ['2nd Expired Agreement''); and

       WHEREAS, the District has allowed the City to continue occupancy and use of the
Property and the City has agreed to maintain the Property and the Covington House on a month­
to-month holdover basis since the expiration date of the 2nd Expired Agreement.

        WHEREAS, the District and the City agree to enter into a long term Use Agreement as
follows:

         NOW THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY AGREED between the City and the District:

         1. The District will allow the City to continue to use and occupy the Property as shown
            in Exhibit "A", until this Agreement is terminated pursuant to Section 8. The Property
            includes those areas customarily used in conjunction with Covington House, such as
            the parking lot and yards surrounding the house.

         2. The District shall receive no compensation from the City for the use of the Property.

         3. The City shall use the Property and Covington House in a manner consistent with the
            current and ongoing use. No significant change in the use of the premises is allowed
            without the District's express written authorization.

         4. The City will maintain Covington House and Property in a condition comparable to its
            condition as of the date of the property exchange.

         5. Throughout the duration of this Agreement, the City agrees to indemnify and hold
            harmless the District from and against all claims, damages, losses, expenses and
            liability, arising out of or resulting from the use of Covington House and the Property
            by the City, including bodily injury and property damaged caused by the City's acts,
            negligence or omissions except for injuries or damages caused by the sole acts or
            negligence of the District.

         6. Throughout the duration of this Agreement, the District agrees to indemnify and hold
            harmless the City from and against all claims, damages, losses, expenses and liability,
            arising out of or resulting from the acts, negligence or omissions of the District,
            including bodily injury and property damage, except for injuries or damages cause by
            the sole acts or negligence of the City.

         7. Assignment and subletting - District's consent required - City shall not assign or
            encumber its interest under this Use Agreement without first obtaining District's
            consent in writing. District shall not unreasonably withhold its consent.

        8. This Use Agreement is for a period of ten (10) years beginning January 1, 2018 and
           shall expire December 31, 2027. The City shall have an option to renew this Use
           Agreement for an additional ten year period provide that notice is given to the District
           at least 90 days prior to the expiration of the previous ten year term. However; this
           Use Agreement shall automatically terminate during the original term or any extension
           period if the City elects to move, destroy, abandon or otherwise discontinue use of the
           Covington House.
