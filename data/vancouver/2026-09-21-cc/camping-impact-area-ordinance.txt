        AN ORDINANCE of the City of Vancouver amending Vancouver Municipal Code

Chapter 8.22 to prohibit camping and outside habitation within 1,500 feet of the City of

Vancouver Bridge Shelter Property; amending VMC 8.22.030 “Definitions,” and VMC 8.22.040

“Unlawful Camping and Outside Habitation;” providing for severability and setting an effective

date.



        WHEREAS, the City of Vancouver is preparing to open the City-owned Bridge Shelter,

which will provide 120 temporary shelter beds along with on-site treatment and supportive

services for adults experiencing homelessness; and

WHEREAS, Vancouver Municipal Code Chapter 8.22 currently designates areas within 1000

feet of Safe Stay Communities as camping and outside habitation impact areas, but the Bridge

Shelter does not fall within the definition of a Safe Stay Community and is therefore not subject

to that existing buffer; and

        WHEREAS, the City has experienced concentrations of camping and outside habitation

in areas near shelter and homeless-service locations, and significant camping currently occurs at

several locations near the Bridge Shelter Property; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds that establishing a camping and outside habitation

impact area surrounding the Bridge Shelter will reduce the likelihood of encampments becoming

concentrated in the area(s) around and adjacent to the facility, support effective access to and

operation of the shelter, and assist the City in mitigating the impacts of unsheltered homelessness

in the surrounding area; and

        WHEREAS, after considering the size and function of the Bridge Shelter, existing

camping conditions in the surrounding area, and alternative buffer distances of 1,000, 1,500 and

2,000 feet, the City Council finds that a 1,500-foot buffer reasonably addresses the identified

conditions without unnecessarily restricting additional areas otherwise available for lawful

nighttime camping and outside habitation.

        Section 2. Section 8.22.030 of the Vancouver Municipal Code entitled “Definitions” as

established by Ordinance M-3323 (1997) and last amended by Ordinance M-4379 § 4, (2022) is

hereby amended to read as follows:



The following definitions are applicable in this chapter unless the context otherwise requires:

“Camp” or “camping” means to pitch, create, use, or occupy camp and outside habitation facilities

for recreational purposes.

“Camp and outside habitation facilities” include, but are not limited to, tents, huts, temporary

shelters, or vehicles.

“Camp and outside habitation paraphernalia” includes, but is not limited to, tarpaulins, cots, beds,

sleeping bags, blankets, mattresses, hammocks, or noncity-designated cooking facilities and

similar equipment.

“Camping and outside habitation impact area” means the areas specified within VMC 8.22.040(B).

“City of Vancouver Bridge Shelter” means the shelter facility that is owned by the City of

Vancouver located at 5313 NE 94th Avenue, Vancouver, Washington 98662

“City of Vancouver Bridge Shelter Property” means the parcel of property where the City of

Vancouver Bridge Shelter is located.

“Family” means two or more “family or household members” as defined by RCW 26.52.010.

“Outside habitation” means to pitch, create, use, or occupy camp and outside habitation facilities

for purposes of shelter for habitation.

“Park” means the same as defined in VMC 15.04.020.

“Safe stay community” means a camp and outside habitation facility for which a permit has been

sought and obtained from the Vancouver city manager, or their designee, pursuant to

VMC 8.22.070(B),      and/or a city-sponsored       safe stay    community      established   under

VMC 8.22.070(F).

“Safe stay community operator” means a person, firm, corporation, or municipal corporation with

a valid safe stay community operator permit issued pursuant to VMC 8.22.070 or, in the case of a

city-sponsored safe stay community, an approved safe stay community facility operator as

designated by the city manager or their designee.

“Specific population safe stay community” means a safe stay community with spaces reserved for

either (a) a single occupant identifying as female; or (b) a family with one or more children under

18 years of age at the time of admission to the safe stay community.

       Section 3. Section 8.22.040 of the Vancouver Municipal Code entitled “Unlawful

camping and outside habitation” as established by Ordinance M-3323 (1997) and last amended

by Ordinance M-4379 § 5, (2022) is hereby amended to read as follows:



       A. Safe Stay Communities. Outside habitation shall be allowed at all times within safe stay

       communities by those residing therein. Residence in a safe stay community shall be at the

       approval of the safe stay community operator.



       B. Camping and Outside Habitation Impact Areas. Except to the extent expressly allowed

       pursuant to subsection A of this section, it shall be unlawful to camp or habitate outside at

       any time within a camping and outside habitation impact area. The following locations are

       camping and outside habitation impact areas:

                1.      Within 1,500 feet of the nearest point of the City of Vancouver Bridge

                Shelter Property.

                1. 2.   Within 1,000 feet of the nearest point of any safe stay community;

                2. 3.   Upon any land used to operate a public water station, wastewater or

                stormwater facility;

      C. Daytime Camping and Outside Habitation Prohibited. Except as otherwise provided

      within this chapter, during the hours of 6:30 a.m. to 9:30 p.m., it shall be unlawful for any

      person to camp, occupy camp and outside habitation facilities for purposes of habitation,

      or use camp and outside habitation paraphernalia in the following areas:

                1. Any park;

                2. Any street; or

                3. Any publicly owned or maintained parking lot or other publicly owned or

                maintained area, improved or unimproved.
