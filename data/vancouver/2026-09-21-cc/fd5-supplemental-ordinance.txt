                  ORDINANCE NO. M- [Ordinance Number]
    AN ORDINANCE relating to the 2025-26 Biennial Budget and making a supplemental

appropriation to account for revenue in excess of initial budget projections and to meet certain

necessary expenditures to be incurred in 2025-26 that were not and could not reasonably have

been foreseen at the time of adoption of the 2025-26 Budget; declaring an emergency and

providing for an immediate effective date.

    WHEREAS, as described in Staff Report ___, City Council has approved an amendment to

an agreement between the City of Vancouver and Clark County Fire District 5 that will fund

seven (7) additional full-time equivalent positions within the Vancouver Fire Department; and

    WHEREAS, City Council finds that it is in the public interest to create and begin hiring for

these additional positions during the remainder of the 2025-26 biennium; and

     WHEREAS, in order to begin implementing the agreement between the City and Clark

County Fire District 5 in the current biennium, certain City budget documents must be amended

to create additional positions and account for the additional revenue and expenses; and

    WHEREAS following the Council Workshop regarding the proposed agreement between

the City of Vancouver and Clark County Fire District 5 that occurred on August 24, 2026, City

Staff has prepared this Supplemental Budget Ordinance for approval; and

    WHEREAS, upon due and proper legal notice, Vancouver City Council held a public

hearing on September 21, 2026, to consider this Supplemental Budget Ordinance.

    NOW, THEREFORE,

ORDINANCE - 1
       BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY OF VANCOUVER:

       Section 1. Supplemental budget appropriation is needed to meet certain necessary

expenditures to be incurred in 2025-26 that were not and could not reasonably have been

foreseen at the time of adoption of the 2025-26 Budget. As required by chapter 35.34 RCW,

there is hereby set forth in summary form the total adjustments of expenditure appropriations for

each separate fund adjusted and the aggregate totals for all such funds combined, as set forth in

Attachment A of this ordinance.

       Section 2. That a total of seven (7) full-time equivalent positions are added, as set forth

in Attachment B to this ordinance. The position adjustments shall be made consistent with

applicable civil service rules and personnel regulations.

       Section 3. The salaries for three (3) new position classifications have been added to the

salary schedule for positions not covered by labor agreements, as set forth in Attachment D of

this ordinance.

       Section 4. The budget amendments and appropriations made in this ordinance are

necessary to meet certain necessary expenditures that will be incurred in 2025-26 biennium that

were not and could not reasonably have been foreseen at the time of adoption of the 2025-26

Budget; an emergency is hereby declared.

       Section 5. Those acts taken pursuant to this ordinance, but prior to its effective date are

hereby ratified and confirmed.

       Section 6. Effective date. This ordinance shall go into effect immediately upon final

passage and publication.
