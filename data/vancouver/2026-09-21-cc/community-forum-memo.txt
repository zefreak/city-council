Youth Community Forum Specifics
      •   Forum will be held from 2pm to 3:30 p.m. at Rooted School Vancouver, 10401 NE
          Fourth Plain Blvd; doors open at 1:30 p.m.
      •   The forum will be the culminating activity of a day-long civic leadership day with
          Rooted students, similar to the City’s earlier Civic Minds program
      •   Activities will include a coffee meeting with the Mayor, sessions with City staff
          and time for students to explore careers in local government
      •   All community members are welcome to attend the forum; however, the civic
          leadership day is only for Rooted students

Meeting Expectations
Independent facilitators from PointNorth will support small-group discussions during
the forum. Across conversations, facilitators will remind participants to:
   • Communicate respectfully: Allow others to speak and listen without interruption
   • Be aware of time: Speak for 1-2 minutes at a time, so everyone has a chance to
       share
   • Stay on topic: Discussions should be focused on the designated topics for each
       table group
   • Be an active participant: All participants are encouraged to listen, ask questions
       and contribute their thoughts and ideas

Draft Discussion Questions
Rooted students identified a wide range of possible discussion topics. We plan to start
with the ice-breaker question:
   • What is your favorite place to go in Vancouver? Why is it special to you?

From there, groups will discuss hopes for the future, topics that are important to them
and community needs:
   • As you think about life after high school, what are your hopes for the future?
   • What issues or topics are most important to students in our community?
         o How are these issues affecting you and people you know?
   • What is something our community needs that we don’t have now?

Community Forum Outcomes
   •      Increase youth engagement to foster a sense of community
   •      Build trust through listening, learning and reflecting together
   •      Gather unique insights from younger community members, including their
          questions, concerns, ideas, and suggestions
