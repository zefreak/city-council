Overview
Background:
• Supplemental Appropriation - a mid-biennial budget amendment that allows
  for necessary adjustments to the adopted biennial budget as a result of
  unanticipated circumstances.
• Council approval is required for increases in appropriations and staffing.




3 Fire District 5 Agreement: Supplemental Budget Ordinance
Fire District 5 Agreement: Supplemental
Budget Ordinance
Overview
Scope:
• The purpose of this ordinance is to align the budget with the recently
  amended services agreement with Fire District 5.
• The ordinance will adjust total appropriation in the Fire Fund, related FTE to
  account for 7.0 limited duration positions, and add new titles to the existing
  non-union & exempt salary schedule.



4 Fire District 5 Agreement: Supplemental Budget Ordinance
Expenditures by Fund Changes
Attachment A




• Per the amended service agreement, Fire District 5 will support 100% of actual costs for 7.0
  new limited duration positions beginning in the fourth quarter of Fiscal Year 2026.

  costs. Funds not needed will not be expended and will not be charged to Fire District 5.
