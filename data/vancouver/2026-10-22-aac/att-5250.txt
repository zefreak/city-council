                                                    Aviation Advisory Committee
                                                    Meeting Minutes

Date: September 9, 2026
Time: 6:00 PM Regular AAC Meeting
Location: 4500 SE Columbia Way, Marine Park Engineering Building

Aviation Advisory Committee (AAC) Members: Chair, Shon Lindley; Vice Chair, Brigham Williams;
Laura Krueger, Matthew Winit, Jordan Thomas, Joanne Landry


1. Call to Order and Roll Call
    • Regular AAC meeting called to order at 6:00 PM by Shon Lindley. The meeting was conducted in a hybrid
         format, with both in-person and remote participants via a Teams video conference.
    • Committee Members Present: Shon Lindley; Brigham Williams; Joanne Landry; Matthew Winit; Jordan
         Thomas
    • City of Vancouver Staff Present: Airport Manager, Meredith Fox; Financial Analyst, Shannon Turk; Public
         Works Director, Steve Wall; Assistant City Attorney, Jonathan Schetky.

2. Community Communications – Public Comment
    • No public comments.

3. Airport Manager’s Updates:
        Airport Master Plan: Meredith
    • The proposed Master Plan schedule was presented at the last meeting. Dates for PAC meetings and
        open house events will be announced once the schedule is finalized.
    • Airport Geographic Information System currently meets all standards.
    • Flight data collection took place on August 31st and is complete.
    • Ground survey will take place later this month and will not affect flights.
    • PAC members will be determined in November.
    • New link on the Be Heard Vancouver webpage on the City’s website for the Master Plan where public
        comments can be made.
        Terminal Renovation: Meredith
    • Exit signage installation in the 105 hangar has been completed.
    • Final punch list walkthrough will be September 23, 2026.
    • September 25th: Project completion with ribbon-cutting ceremony and a potluck.
        2018 Audit Follow-Up: Meredith
    • The 2018 corrective action plan was reviewed by the City’s auditor and legal department, which
        determined that the City has been following practices as suggested by the external auditor in 2018.
              • Contract compliance reviews have been completed.
              • The City continues to improve oversight of subleasing on land leases.
              • Currently working on improving hangar waitlist procedures.
              • Financial planning and rate analysis: rental rates and fee structures will be evaluated as part
                  of the Master Plan process.
              • Airport Staffing: A new position is being added to the airport staff.

6. Proposed Updates to VMC 10.05.04: Presented by Steve Wall
    • Vancouver Municipal Code (VMC) 10.05.04 is specific to the AAC.

        changes. AAC members collaboratively reviewed and discussed the proposed changes.

        requesting to review the revised VMC document before being presented to council.

7. AAC Communications
    • The AAC meeting in October will be held on Thursday, October 22, 2026.
