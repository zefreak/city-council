## Noted

covering litigation the agency reasonably believes may be commenced against it. "No after action"
means no vote is anticipated in open session. There is no public comment item on the agenda.

This is a **special meeting** — RCW 42.30.080 requires only 24 hours' notice — which is the channel
that most easily passes unnoticed. In this case there is nothing behind it that is public.
