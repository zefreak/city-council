### 3a — Proposed Council action to amend the Cultural Access ordinance (30 min, no attachment)

What is already on the record (10 September brief, from the 13 August minutes and the programme
site):

- Changing the year-one category allocations *"would require City Council approval of an amendment
  before the award period in October."* Awards are due **mid-October**.
- Changing the fiscal-sponsor fee cap (up to 10%) would also need Council action.

So this may be the amendment the Commission was told it would need to change allocations before
awards go out. **That is a guess, and is labelled as one.** Nothing on this agenda says which provision
is being amended.

### 3b — Questions for Madrona Law (20 min, no attachment)

### 3c — Grant review and approval process (deck read)

The deck is procedural. The Grant Review Committee scores, ranks and draws a funding line. It also
recommends the **flexible allocation** ($700,000 in year one) *"based on budget need, geographic equity
and access, and high-scoring proposals ranked below a category's funding limit."* The Commission
*"will not discuss individual grants"*. It votes to accept the ranked lists or amend them. **Proposals
below the line "cannot be funded with year one allocations."** That makes the flexible pot the only
discretionary money in the round, which is where an equity argument for a below-line applicant has to
be made. No application counts are in the deck, despite the item title.
