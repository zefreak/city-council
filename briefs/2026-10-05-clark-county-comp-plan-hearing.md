**What to say.** The 1 October brief sets out the four arguments with page references. Short version:

1. Exhibit 4, Table 3, is the County's own memo. It shows **Camas +2,210, Ridgefield +1,379 and
   La Center +245** units over their 20-year allocations, and all three are still asking to expand.

   written in. **It is not an offset.** By design it is no longer tied to the expansions.

## Money and land

No appropriation is before the Council. What is at stake is land. Five agricultural de-designations
feed four urban growth area expansions in three cities. The plan also re-sets the traffic impact fee
schedule, roughly two to three times current rates (1 October brief, item 5). A related $3,648 consent
item for the plan's environmental consultant is on the **6 October** agenda.
