# Clark County — Wednesday 9 September 2026: everything cancelled

Both Wednesday sessions are off. From the county's own meeting listing:

- **Work Sessions, 9:00am–noon** — "Work Sessions scheduled for September 9 have been cancelled
  due to lack of a quorum."
- **Council Time** — "the Council Time meeting scheduled for September 9, 2026 has been cancelled
  due to lack of a quorum."

The Weekly Calendar PDF for the week of 6 September (posted the preceding Thursday, cached at
`data/clark-county/weekly-calendar/weekly-2026-09-06.txt`) confirms both and lists only one other
item all week: **C-TRAN Board of Directors, Tuesday 8 September 5:30pm**, at the C-TRAN
Administration Offices, 10600 NE 51st Circle. Monday 7 September is the Labor Day holiday and
county offices are closed.

## Bottom line

Nothing is being decided, but something is being lost. Council Time is the weekly meeting and the
only Wednesday public-comment slot, and it is the meeting at which the Council's **comprehensive
plan adoption hearing dates were set on 26 August** — dates that as of 4 September appear in no
published notice. Cancelling 9 September removes the last routine opportunity to confirm them
before they arrive.

## Why the timing matters

The Council agreed at Council Time on 26 August to start its comprehensive plan hearing **Thursday
1 October at 4pm**, continue **Friday 2 October at 10am**, and continue again **Monday 5 October at
10am** if needed. That was an item added to the agenda the same morning and it exists only in the
minutes. Meanwhile:

- The county's **comp-plan events calendar**
  (`/community-planning/2025-update-meeting-and-event-information`), checked 4 September 2026, has
  **no entry later than 15 September**. The October hearings are not on it.
- The **5 August news release** still gives 6 October for the hearing and 13 October for adoption.
- The Planning Commission's decision table, updated 27 August, still heads its column "Council
  Decision 10/13/2026."
- The one *published document* that corroborates an October adoption is the staff report for
  Resolution 2026-09-01 on the **15 September** Council agenda, which says the periodic update is
  "now expected to be approved in **mid-October 2026**" — and even that gives no hearing dates.

So the adoption hearings for a 20-year comprehensive plan are four weeks out, three of the four
published sources disagree with the Council's own decision, and the weekly meeting where it would
be corrected is cancelled. **The next scheduled public meeting of the Council is 15 September, and
it carries Open Public Comment on any county business.** That is the place to ask.

## Watch

**A quorum failure on a five-member council means three of five councilors were unavailable on a
normal Wednesday.** That is worth noting once and not more: early September, immediately after
Labor Day, is an ordinary time for it. It becomes a pattern only if it repeats. The 1 September
regular meeting went ahead and the 15 September one is agendaed, so business is moving.

Two items that were live at Council Time and now have nowhere to land before 15 September:

- **Letter of Concurrence — NW 31st Ave Safety Improvements (HSIP)**, on the 2 September agenda.
- **Work session request: Public Works, Parks and Nature draft 2027–2032 Capital Improvement
  Plan**, requested for **14 October**. A work session takes **no public comment**, and it precedes
  a hearing that would adopt the CIP and amend both the Parks, Recreation and Open Space Plan and
  the Natural Areas Acquisition Plan.

Also still unnoticed: a Clark County Council **Strategic Planning meeting on 14 September**,
referenced twice in the 26 August Council Time minutes as the destination for a Mental Health Sales
Tax Advisory Board membership question and an Opioid Abatement Council staffing item. It appears on
no agenda, calendar or listing, including the Weekly Calendar for that week. If it is a special
meeting, RCW 42.30.080 requires only 24 hours' notice.

## What I could not check

- **Whether the cancelled Council Time business is being rescheduled**, and to when. No revised or

- **Whether the 14 September Strategic Planning meeting exists.** It is named only in minutes.
- **Whether the October comp plan hearing dates have been noticed anywhere I did not look.** The
  two Community Planning pages, the Council meetings listing and the Weekly Calendar were all
