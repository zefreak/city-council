## Update, 9 October — packet published, still no applications

The packet (meeting file 3073, cached at `data/vancouver/2026-10-14-ltac/packet-3073.txt`) is five
pages: the agenda, comment instructions and the 15 July minutes. **It contains no applicant list, scores
or dollar figures**, so the grant review itself is still not visible before the meeting. Two things in
the minutes are worth a note:

- **Visit Vancouver proposed that its funding become a fixed percentage of prior-year lodging tax
  collections**, instead of an annual request (15 July minutes, Community Communications). Locking a
  share in removes the destination marketing organisation from the yearly competition that every other
  applicant faces. Watch for it in the recommendations to Council.
- **The committee kept equity as a scoring criterion** but de-weighted alignment with the Destination
  Master Plan and "uniqueness". Paid lodging nights remain the most heavily weighted criterion. The
  committee said very low equity scores *"may warrant additional Committee discussion"*.

Schedule from the minutes: applicant presentations on 21 October, 29 October (may be cancelled) and
