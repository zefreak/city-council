## Watch

**Three cancellations in a fortnight is the only thing here worth noticing**, and it is worth
noticing lightly — advisory bodies routinely go dark in late August, and two of the three have
quorum-sized memberships that make August hard. It is not evidence of anything on its own. What
would make it evidence is a fourth, or a pattern of business piling up.

Two of the three do handle real material when they sit:

- **Lodging Tax Advisory Committee** recommends how lodging tax revenue is allocated. RCW 67.28.1817
  requires a municipality to submit proposed lodging-tax uses to its LTAC for comment before acting.
  A cancelled LTAC in September is worth tracking against the city budget calendar, because
  award recommendations have to happen somewhere. **Flagged from the calendar entry alone — no
  agenda was published, so I do not know what, if anything, was scheduled to be on it.**
- **Transportation and Mobility Commission** advises on transportation policy — which is adjacent
  to both the impact-fee work at the Planning Commission and Clark County's coming micromobility
  policy work session.

**Parking Advisory Committee, 9 September, 8:00am** is the one still on. No agenda. Parking is not
a small item in this cycle: HB 1491's mandated offsets include **no parking minimums** near BRT
stations, and the Planning Commission takes the Title 20 HB 1491 hearing the day before, on
8 September. Whether those two touch is not knowable until the agenda posts. **Flagged on the
calendar entry alone.**

The **25 August Planning Commission** cancellation does not appear to have moved business: the
8 September Planning Commission agenda was published on 21 August, before the cancellation, and
has not changed since (unchanged fingerprint in `data/.agenda-watch-state.json`).
