## Bottom line

Two things, and the second was missed the first time. The Mental Health Sales Tax advisory board is
being re-established with **no seat for anyone who uses the services it funds**. And the county is
about to put **Tri-Mountain Golf Course out to a lease of up to 50 years** in which the payment to
the public is worth **10 points out of 100**.

## Act on this

### 1. Tri-Mountain Golf Course lease RFP — released 9 September, proposals due 13 October

Presented by Michelle Schuster, Director of Internal Services. Under **old business**, which is why
it looked informational. It is not: the RFP has a release date eight days out.

**What is being let.** A lease of a **county-owned** golf course to a private operator for
"management, operations, and maintenance… with minimal County operational support."

| Term | As presented |
|---|---|
| Initial term | **10 years**, 1 Jan 2027 – 31 Dec 2036 |
| Extensions | County reserves the right to extend **four additional 10-year terms** |
| Maximum exposure | **50 years** on the same or mutually agreed terms |
| Termination | County may terminate on **90 days' notice**, "solely in the county's judgment," if lease requirements are not met |

**The scoring is the finding.** 100 points before interviews:

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Operating Plan | 25 |
| Proposal Approach / Quality | 20 |
| Creativity / Experience | 15 |
| Work History / References | 15 |
| Capital Improvements / Maintenance | 15 |
| **Lease Payment to County** | **10** |
| Interview / presentation (finalists only) | **+50**, for a total of 150 |

So the **money the public receives is the lowest-weighted of the six criteria** — 10% of the base
score, 6.7% once interviews are counted — while the county's own stated goals include "**maximizing
the return on its assets**." Those two things do not fit together, and the mismatch is the question
to put to Internal Services. The **interview alone is worth 50 points**, five times the lease
payment and a third of the total, which is a large discretionary component in awarding a lease that
can run half a century.

Two goals in the deck also pull against each other and should be named: "a quality, family friendly
experience at **affordable rates** for the citizens of the Clark County" and "**maximizing the
return** on its assets." Whichever way the county resolves that, the resolution belongs in the RFP
rather than in negotiation after selection.

**The timeline, and there is no public comment step anywhere in it:**

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| RFP released | **9 September 2026** |
| Pre-submittal meeting | 18 September |
| Deadline for questions | 25 September |
| Final addendum | 2 October |
| **Proposals due** | **13 October 2026** |
| Evaluation | 14–21 October |
| Interviews, if held | 26–29 October |
| Selection committee recommendation | **30 October** |
| Contract negotiation and execution | 1–17 November |
| Lease intended to begin | 1 January 2027 |

**No council vote and no public hearing appear on that timeline** — it runs from selection committee
straight to negotiation and execution. Whether the lease returns to Council for approval is not
stated in the deck. That is the thing to ask, and the window to ask it in is now, because after
9 September the terms are fixed in a released RFP and after 13 October the field is closed.

**Where to raise it:** Tuesday open public comment takes any matter of county business, agenda or
not — **1 September, 10am** is the last Tuesday meeting before the RFP is released. Three minutes.

### 2. Item 5.2 — Mental Health Sales Tax: Resolution Creating Advisory Board + Bylaws

Council Time takes public comment, 3 minutes, but **only on items already on the agenda**, and you
must name the specific item when you speak. This one qualifies. You cannot yield your time to
another speaker without a majority vote of the Council (Rules §VII.L.iii, §VII.L.v).

The bylaws set membership at eight voting seats:

| Seat | Count |
|---|---|
| Superior Court Judge | 1 |
| District Court Administrator | 1 |
| Juvenile Court Administrator | 1 |
| Community Services Director | 1 |
| CS Mental Health/Substance Abuse Program Manager | 1 |
| Public Health Director | 1 |
| County Councilors (rural + urban) | 2 |

Every seat is a county official or a judge. **There is no seat for a peer specialist, a service
user, a community provider, or a member of the public.** Three of the eight are courts. The chair
and vice-chair are both councilors (Article V), so the board cannot elect its own leadership.

The board's function is to shape the County Manager's recommended budget for the 0.1% treatment
sales tax Clark County has levied since 2007 under RCW 82.14.460 (authorised by E2SSB-5763, 2005).
It meets **at least annually** (Article IV.1) — that is the floor, and it is a low one for a body
overseeing a permanent countywide tax.

The obvious asks: add service-user and peer seats; separate the chair from the Council; require
quarterly rather than annual meetings.

**One caveat, and it matters.** The resolution attached to this agenda is the **original staff
report dated 27 February 2018** (Resolution 2018-02-18), not a 2026 document. I could not
determine from the attachments what the 2026 item actually changes — whether it re-adopts the
2018 structure unchanged, or amends it. The membership list above is from the bylaws as attached.
**Confirm the current proposal before quoting the composition as newly adopted.**

## Money and land

**Tri-Mountain Golf Course**: a county-owned course going out to lease for an initial **10 years**
with **four 10-year extension options**, i.e. up to **50 years**. **No dollar figure is published** —
"total lease terms and payments to the County, including extensions, will be negotiated determined
by evaluating the selected proposal(s)," so the price is an output of the process rather than an
input to it. That is the one number a reader most wants and it does not exist yet. Payment for
option terms is "reviewed prior to extension."

Otherwise nothing with a dollar figure. The MHST item governs how a standing revenue stream is
allocated but does not itself allocate.
