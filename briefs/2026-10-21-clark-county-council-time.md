## Act on this

### 8.3 — 2027 Legislative Session Agenda Conversation (no attachment)

**Background, from the 7 October minutes.** Policy analyst Jordan Boege explained that HB 1217 caps
annual lot rent increases in mobile home parks at **5%**, and that the cap *"does not apply when a
resident moves out"*. Yung asked for the County to advocate an amendment that would *"remove mobile
home parks from the carve out"*. The Council *"supported having this item come back with more
detail"*, and Boege is drafting it with Yung. The same minutes record Yung attending a mobile home park
residents' meeting on Saturday 3 October.

**Why it matters.** Under vacancy decontrol, a park owner can reset lot rent without limit at
turnover. A manufactured home is very hard to move, so a resident who sells is selling into a reset
rent, and the reset depresses the home's sale price. That turns the 5% cap into an incentive to push
turnover. **Closing the carve-out is a clear, winnable tenant protection**, and it has a Councilor
sponsor already.

**What to do.** Speak under **item 8.3** for three minutes, or write in. Say: put the mobile home park
rent-cap amendment on the County's 2027 legislative agenda, as drafted with Councilor Yung. Comment is
limited to agenda items, so name 8.3. Written comment goes to
https://clark.wa.gov/councilors/public-comment. **This is most effective coming from park residents
themselves.**

*Firm vs not:* the 7 October discussion is in the minutes and firm. Whether the MHP item is in the 8.3
discussion is **not known**. There is no attachment.
