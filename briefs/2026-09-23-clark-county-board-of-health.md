## Bottom line

**A consent item added at 12:30pm on Monday would give the Director of Public Health standing
authority to contract outside Health Officers "when necessary", with no dollar cap, no term and no
figures in any of the staff report's budget tables — and the department's own form marks the item as
a hearing, not consent.** It is a small, probably sensible piece of continuity cover for an Interim
Health Officer's planned holiday. It is also an open-ended delegation, and it is going through the

## Act on this

**Comments from the Public, straight after roll call.** Three minutes. If you want the Health Officer
delegation discussed, that is the moment — or ask a councillor to pull it from consent.

Two questions with documentary backing:

1. **Is this item consent or hearing?** The staff report's own checkbox block reads
   `☐ Consent / ☒ Hearing / ☐ County Manager`. The published agenda lists it under **Consent
   Agenda**. Somebody moved it, and the form was not updated. Ask which is intended.
2. **What is the scope of the delegation?** The requested action is approval "for the Director of
   Public Health to authorize the contract with qualified Health Officers **when necessary** to cover
   for our Board of Health appointed Clark County Health Officer." No dollar limit, no maximum
   duration, no requirement to report back. The background describes a specific, bounded need — the
   Interim Health Officer has a planned vacation — but the authority sought is standing and
   open-ended. Those are not the same thing, and narrowing it to a dollar cap and an annual
   report-back would cost nothing.

### Health Officer Coverage, in full

From the staff report (Department of Public Health, dated 23 September 2026, signed off by Deputy
County Manager April Furth):

> "Clark County has historically maintained both a Health Officer and a Deputy Health Officer to
> ensure continuity of public health leadership and adequate coverage during periods of absence.
> With the recent retirement of the Health Officer and the appointment of an Interim Health Officer,
> the department has continued its commitment to maintaining stable and compliant public health
> oversight. The Interim Health Officer has a planned vacation, and additional coverage is needed…"

**Read what that paragraph concedes.** The county's stated historic practice is a Health Officer
*and* a Deputy. It currently has neither — it has an Interim Health Officer and no deputy, which is
why a single planned holiday requires a Board resolution. RCW 70.05.050 requires every local board
of health to employ and appoint a qualified local health officer; there is no statutory slack here.
The item is the symptom. **The question worth asking is the recruitment timeline for a permanent
Health Officer and a Deputy**, which the staff report does not address — "Prior council
discussions/direction/approval: N/A".

**The budget section is empty.** The report ticks "Action falls within existing budget capacity" and
says "We have existing funds in our services budget to cover this upcoming coverage need", then
leaves every field of the Budget Details table — dollar amount, fund, cost center, BASUB, program —
blank, along with all three years of the estimated revenue and expenditure tables. Per
`BRIEF-PROMPT.md` §3c: a blank field is not evidence of no fiscal impact.
